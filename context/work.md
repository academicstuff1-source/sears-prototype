# Work and Business Context

## Business Overview
Independent AI automation consulting practice. Business name TBD.

**Stage:** Early validation. No stable client revenue yet. Goal is to close the first paid engagement, prove measurable value, and learn which offer, customer type, and workflow problem are easiest to sell and deliver.

**Preferred target:** Business owners, operators, and managers with urgent, expensive, repetitive, or frustrating workflow problems — any niche, with healthcare as a preferred starting focus.

## Services

| Service | Description |
|---|---|
| AI automation consulting | Help businesses identify repetitive, error-prone, or slow workflows that could be improved with AI or automation |
| Workflow discovery & process mapping | Understand how a business operates, identify bottlenecks, and prioritize improvement opportunities |
| Automation design & implementation | Build practical low-code, no-code, or AI-assisted workflows that improve speed, consistency, follow-up, or reporting |
| AI tool evaluation & strategy | Help businesses decide which AI tools are worth using and how to integrate them into existing operations |
| Rapid prototypes & proof of concepts | Small demonstrations that show how a workflow could work before committing to a larger implementation |
| Ongoing support (potential) | Optimization, maintenance, training, documentation, and additional improvements after initial projects |

## Highest Leverage Right Now
1. Find businesses with real, urgent, solvable workflow problems
2. Define a simple, credible offer a prospect can understand and buy
3. Start qualified conversations through outreach, networking, referrals, and prospecting
4. Build enough implementation capability to deliver a strong first result
5. Turn the first engagement into a case study, testimonial, and repeatable offer

## Revenue Stage
Early validation. Near-term milestone: close one paid pilot, audit, implementation project, or recurring support engagement. Use that result to improve pricing, positioning, and outreach.

## Compliance and Security Constraints
- Respect client confidentiality, data privacy, intellectual property, and secure handling of business information.
- Do not place credentials, API keys, passwords, tokens, private client data, or sensitive data in tracked project files.
- Do not claim a workflow is legally compliant, secure, or suitable for a regulated industry unless verified.
- Flag situations involving financial data, health data, employee data, customer personal data, contracts, legal requirements, or security-sensitive systems.
- Never imply AI output is guaranteed correct. Include human review where errors could cause material harm.
- Do not make commitments about timelines, pricing, scope, results, or integrations without approval.
