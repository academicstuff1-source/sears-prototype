# Current Priorities

> Last updated: 2026-08-26

## Number-One Priority
Get 5 conversations with 5 real business owners — any niche, preferred: healthcare. Optimizing for reps and pattern recognition.

## Active Focus Areas
1. **Find qualified prospects** — businesses with urgent, expensive, repetitive, or frustrating workflow problems that are realistically solvable
2. **Define a simple, clear offer** — scope, problem, outcome, delivery approach, pricing hypothesis, and next step a prospect can understand and buy
3. **Build outreach and prospecting rhythm** — repeatable process for target selection, research, outreach, and follow-up with CRM discipline
4. **Develop implementation capability** — practical AI, automation, no-code, and low-code skills that can produce real client results
5. **Build repeatable systems** — templates, SOPs, and documentation to execute faster and scale after the first client

## Greatest Near-Term Impact
Getting qualified conversations with prospects and converting one into a first paid engagement. This validates the offer, creates revenue, reveals what buyers care about, and creates a real-world delivery opportunity.

## Time-Sensitive Items
No fixed external deadlines. Working urgency: establish a consistent outreach rhythm, learn from real buyer conversations, identify a high-value solvable problem, and close the first paid engagement as soon as possible.

## Priority Order (When Tasks Compete)
1. Protect trust, privacy, security, and reputation
2. Activities most likely to create qualified sales conversations and win the first customer
3. Work that clarifies, validates, or strengthens the initial offer
4. Client delivery work and commitments
5. High-leverage systems, templates, SOPs, and automation capabilities
6. Learning that directly improves near-term sales or delivery results
7. Low-leverage optimization, polishing, speculative tool research, and nonessential projects

## What Not to Prioritize Right Now
- Premature scaling, automation infrastructure, or complex tooling before the first client
- Brand building, social media, or content marketing before client conversations are producing signal
- Locking into a single niche before enough discovery conversations have revealed patterns
