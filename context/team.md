# Team Context

## Team Structure
Currently operating as a solo founder. Michael owns strategy, sales, outreach, discovery, offer development, technical implementation, client communication, and operations.

## Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | When to Involve |
|---|---|---|
| Michael (Founder) | Owns everything | Always — all decisions, approvals, and external actions |
| Prospects | Business owners, operators, managers, department leaders with potential workflow problems | During outreach, qualification, and discovery |
| Clients | Businesses engaged for workflow discovery, automation, prototypes, implementation, or support | During delivery, communication, and follow-up |
| Future contractors/specialists | Technical development, automation, design, security, legal, domain expertise | When scope exceeds solo capacity |
| Referral partners & advisors | Sources of introductions, market feedback, credibility, strategic guidance | Proactively as relationships develop |

## Where Work Happens

| System | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Google Workspace / Gmail | Documents, files, calendar, email — primary working environment |
| HubSpot | Prospect records, outreach tracking, pipeline management, follow-up tasks |
| Google Antigravity | Project planning, prototypes, workspace management, documentation, AI-assisted work |

## Main Bottleneck
As a solo founder: balancing business development, prospecting, sales conversations, offer design, learning, technical delivery, and administrative work — while keeping attention on the activities most likely to produce the first customer.

## Delegation and Escalation
- The assistant may draft, research, organize, prepare plans, and recommend next steps without approval.
- All external actions, communications, purchases, CRM changes, and client-facing work require Michael's explicit approval before execution.
- No contractors or advisors are currently active. Note any that are added.
