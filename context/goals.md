# Goals - Q3 2026

> Update this file at the start of each quarter.
> Note: No formal OKRs or revenue targets set yet. Goals below are informal but directionally clear.

## Quarterly Goals

- [ ] Identify promising customer types or market segments without prematurely locking into one industry
- [ ] Define a simple, credible initial service offer that solves a concrete workflow or operational problem
- [ ] Build a repeatable prospect research, outreach, and follow-up system
- [ ] Have enough discovery conversations to learn which problems are urgent, common, budgeted, and feasible to solve
- [ ] Close the first paid client engagement, pilot, audit, prototype, or implementation project
- [ ] Deliver a useful result and turn it into a case study, testimonial, referral opportunity, or repeatable service package
- [ ] Build foundational documentation for sales, delivery, tool evaluation, and project execution

## Milestones

| Milestone | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 5 conversations with real business owners | Not started | Top priority — any niche, healthcare preferred |
| First paid engagement closed | Not started | Pilot, audit, implementation, or support |
| First client result delivered | Not started | Measurable outcome, not just deliverable |
| Case study or testimonial captured | Not started | Needed for outreach credibility |
| Repeatable outreach system documented | Not started | SOP + CRM workflow |

## Measures of Success
- At least 5 qualified discovery conversations completed
- First paid engagement closed (any structure)
- Clear understanding of which problems are easiest to sell and deliver
- A documented case study or strong testimonial from first client work

## Risks and Constraints
- Solo capacity — every hour spent on low-leverage work is an hour not spent on client conversations
- Offer clarity — unclear positioning makes outreach harder and conversion lower
- No inbound leads yet — all traction depends on outbound effort and referral development
- Implementation confidence — must be honest about scope limits with early clients

## Informal Longer-Term Direction
- After first client: improve pricing, positioning, and outreach based on real data
- Build a small portfolio of repeatable service packages around proven problem types
- Evaluate whether to specialize in healthcare or follow the strongest signal
