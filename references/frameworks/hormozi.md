# Alex Hormozi Frameworks ($100M Offers & Leads)

This document distills Alex Hormozi's principles most relevant to Michael's current stage: getting conversations, qualifying pain, and closing the first client.

## 1. The Value Equation (from *$100M Offers*)
Formula: `(Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)`

**Applied meaning:** Every message, call, and offer must maximize the top and minimize the bottom.
- **Make the result vivid:** What is the exact dream outcome?
- **Make the proof credible:** Why should they believe we can do it?
- **Make the timeline short:** How fast can they see a result?
- **Make the ask frictionless:** Remove risk and effort from the prospect's plate.

## 2. Grand Slam Offer Construction (from *$100M Offers*)
Make the offer so specific and valuable that saying no feels stupid.
- Lead with the problem and name the exact cost of inaction.
- Define a clear, measurable outcome.
- Remove the risk entirely.
- **Applied to Michael's context:** A discovery call shouldn't feel like a sales pitch; it should feel like free consulting or a valuable diagnostic session.

## 3. Lead Generation Hierarchy (from *$100M Leads*)
- **Warm outreach first:** People you know, adjacent networks, referrals.
- **Content and engagement second:** Comment, add value publicly, DM from a warm signal.
- **Cold outreach third:** Only after warm channels are exhausted or insufficient.
- **The "give, give, give, ask" rule:** Provide observable value before making any ask.

## 4. The Hormozi DM Framework (from *$100M Leads* + social content)
- **First message goal:** Get a reply, not a meeting.
- **Specificity over flattery:** Reference something exact, not generic compliments.
- **The "free value first" play:** Offer a specific insight or observation with no strings attached.
- **The soft CTA:** End with a low-friction question that is easy to say yes to.

## 5. Communication Principles (from social content)
- **Be direct:** Say the exact thing. Remove hedging language (e.g., "I think", "maybe").
- **Specificity is proof of expertise:** Vague claims kill credibility. Use numbers and concrete examples.
- **Volume and consistency beat perfection:** Ship the message, learn from the response, iterate.
- **The goal of the first touch is curiosity, not conversion.**

## 6. Relevant Quotes / Anchors
- *"Make people an offer so good they feel stupid saying no."*
- *"The niche you pick is less important than the pain you solve."*
- *"The first conversation is not a sales call. It's a diagnostic."*
- *"Vague goals produce vague results. Specific pain closes deals."*
