## 3. Decisions & Epiphanies Captured

- **Web Scraping is Not the Lever:** CRO portals (Fortrea et al.) are notoriously outdated. The "hidden" trials are only discoverable by calling the recruitment lines directly. Scraping won't help. The bottleneck is administrative friction on the back end, not data access.

- **The Real Bottleneck is Data Entry:** Christopher is acting as his own manual data entry clerk. Every call requires note-taking, formatting, and manual Telegram posting. Eliminating that friction — via voice memo → AI transcription → structured internal record — is the automation value.

- **The Auto-Broadcast Architecture is Legally Broken:** Any Telegram post with eligibility criteria, compensation, and a CTA is classified as IRB-regulated recruitment advertising. Publishing automatically from a phone call, without written site authorization, creates material compliance exposure. The safe system requires a human approval gate before every publish.

- **The Business Model is B2B, Not Consumer Subscriptions:** The Telegram group is a proof-of-concept asset. The real buyers are research sites, CROs, and recruitment vendors paying per consented referral, per completed prescreen, or on monthly retainer. Antidote, SubjectWell/Reify, and Clariness validate this model at scale.

- **The Big 6 CRO Landscape:** IQVIA (#1), ICON plc, PPD (Thermo Fisher), Syneos Health, Parexel, and Fortrea are the primary targets. Christopher's proven methodology can scale horizontally across all six once the DFW pipeline is proven.
