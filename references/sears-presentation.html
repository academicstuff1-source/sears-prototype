Compliance Reality

The IRB Line You Cannot Cross

⚠️

What Triggers IRB Review

Any public post that contains eligibility criteria + compensation + a call-to-action is legally classified as recruitment advertising — even if the information came from a public call center.

Auto-publishing AI-generated posts without written site authorization = material compliance exposure.

🛡️

The Simple Fix

One human approval step between AI draft and publish. The technology handles everything before and after that click — you just authorize each post.

This transforms the liability into a competitive moat. Sites trust you because you operate like a compliant partner, not a scrapers.

Never use: "hidden trials" · "secret inventory" · "exclusive access" · "we can get you enrolled" · "we know you qualify"

Revenue Model

How You Make Money (The B2B Stack)

1

Campaign Setup Fee

Charge each research site or CRO a flat fee to onboard their approved study materials, format them for your audience, and configure the referral tracking loop. Low-friction first transaction.

Immediate — \$200–\$1,000/study
2

Fee per Consented Referral

Every time a Telegram member explicitly opts-in to be contacted by an official study team, you bill the site. Companies like Antidote and SubjectWell charge \$50–\$500+ per qualified referral at scale.

Performance-based — scales with audience
3

Monthly Retainer

After proving consistent referral quality, transition sites to a monthly retainer for priority campaign management and exclusive audience access. This is the recurring revenue flywheel.

Target: \$500–\$3,000/site/month

Key insight: You don't charge the participants. You charge the multi-million dollar research organizations that are desperate for qualified leads.
