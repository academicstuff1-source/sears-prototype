# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Jesse Summerlin at Dwell Roofing & Exteriors

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Jesse Summerlin, Owner
- **The Angle:** Roofing in DFW. High volume of leads, particularly during storm seasons. Challenges likely include missed calls from frantic homeowners, keeping CRM updated, and scheduling inspections without double-booking.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Jesse, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with other local trade owners in DFW. A massive problem roofing owners face is missing new customer calls while they are out doing inspections or on a roof. It's usually costing them lucrative storm damage jobs because the homeowner just calls the next roofer on Google."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what happens to a new lead when they call Dwell Roofing and you're in the middle of an inspection?"*

## 3. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(CRITICAL RULE: Always verify identity first. Ask "Is this Jesse?" Owner-operators often answer the phones themselves).*

**The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Jesse around, or is he out on a roof doing an inspection right now?"*
**The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with independent roofing owners to build operational playbooks. I imagine as the one managing the front, it gets incredibly chaotic for you fielding frantic storm damage calls and chasing down leads when Jesse is unavailable?"*
**The Ask for Access:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with him before the next big storm hits. What is the best way for me to catch Jesse so I can show him how to take that lead leakage completely off your plate?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"When an emergency roof repair lead calls and you can't answer, what exactly happens? Do they leave a voicemail, or do they just move down the list?"*
2. *"How much time do you spend at the end of the day calling back numbers, only to find out they already scheduled an inspection with someone else?"*
3. *"If you did nothing about missed calls for the next 12 months, what does that actually cost you in lost revenue?"*
4. *"What would it mean for your business if every missed call was instantly texted back to capture the lead's information?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I already use JobNimbus / AccuLynx."** | *"That makes total sense. We don't replace that—we build the 'glue' that automatically texts missed calls within 30 seconds and feeds them straight into your CRM. How well is your current setup stopping lead leakage when you're busy?"* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means you're likely missing calls that could be high-ticket jobs. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest bottleneck right now: missing incoming calls, or chasing down unpaid invoices?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how an owner-operator solved this exact missed-call issue. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on Tuesday?"*
