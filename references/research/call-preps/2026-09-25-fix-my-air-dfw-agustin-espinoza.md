# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Agustin Espinoza at Fix My Air DFW

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Agustin Espinoza, Owner
- **The Angle:** Owner-operated HVAC in Fort Worth. He personally runs most service calls. If he's on a roof or in an attic, he can't answer the phone. Missed calls = lost jobs to bigger competitors. 

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Agustin, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with other local trade owners in DFW. A massive problem owner-operators face is missing new customer calls while they are out on a job or in an attic. It's usually costing them emergency repair jobs because the customer just calls the next HVAC company on Google."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what happens to a new lead when they call Fix My Air and you're in the middle of a repair?"*

## 3. The Gatekeeper Play (Turning Reception into an Influencer)
*(If the owner isn't available and you hit a receptionist/dispatcher)*

### Phase 1: Validate Their Pain
> *"Are you the one managing the phones and the dispatch board today? ... Man, God bless you. You guys have the hardest job in the company right now."*

### Phase 2: Pitch to *Their* Problem
> *"I actually called to speak with Agustin, but I build tools specifically for **you**. I know that when the techs are out on a roof and you're stuck on the other line, emergency calls slip to voicemail. How much of your day is spent calling those numbers back just to find out they already hired another company?"*

### Phase 3: The "Magic Wand" & Recruit
> *"I set up a system where, the second you miss a call because you're busy, it instantly texts the customer back to capture the lead. It saves you from having to chase down voicemails all afternoon. If I put together a quick 5-minute visual of how this works, would you be willing to put it in front of Agustin for me?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"When an emergency AC repair lead calls and you can't answer, what exactly happens? Do they leave a voicemail, or do they just move down the list?"*
2. *"How much time do you spend at the end of the day calling back numbers, only to find out they already hired someone else?"*
3. *"If you did nothing about missed calls for the next 12 months, what does that actually cost you in lost dispatch revenue?"*
4. *"What would it mean for your business if every missed call was instantly texted back with a booking link?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I already use ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro."** | *"That makes total sense. We don't replace that—we build the 'glue' that automatically texts missed calls within 30 seconds and feeds them straight into your software. How well is your current setup stopping lead leakage when you're busy?"* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means you're likely missing calls that could be high-ticket jobs. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest bottleneck right now: missing incoming calls, or chasing down unpaid invoices?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how an owner-operator solved this exact missed-call issue. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on Tuesday?"*
