# 📞 Call Battlecard: Mark Leal (State Farm)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Mark Leal, Agency Owner (Arlington & Euless)
- **The Angle:** He runs two offices. His team (Sam, Amanda, Aya) is busy handling repetitive service/billing calls instead of writing new business, and lead handoffs across two locations likely fall through the cracks.
- **The Entry:** Warm connection (same church).

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (Amanda, Sam, Aya, or Nohemi)

### Route A: The Bypass (Default)
*Goal: Sound like a peer. Use vague truth to lower their guard.*
- **The Ask:** *"Hey [Name], this is Michael Anyanwu. Could you grab Mark for me?"*
- **If they ask "What is this regarding?":** *"Yeah, of course. Mark and I go to the same church, and I'm calling to connect with him on some local business operations. Is he in the Arlington office today, or over in Euless?"*
- **If they say he is busy / want to take a message:** *"No problem, I know he's busy running both offices. I actually have a quick visual map I wanted to send over to him regarding how some local agencies are handling call routing. What's the best direct email to get that on his desk?"*

### Route B: The Influencer Pivot (If they are chatty/stressed)
*Goal: Turn them into a champion by offering to take annoying work off their plate. Do NOT mention AI or they will feel threatened.*
- **The Pivot:** *"Ah, no worries. To be honest [Name], I'm working with a few local owners on a system that intercepts all those repetitive billing and policy-change calls so the team doesn't have to deal with them all day. As someone on the front lines, is the call volume for basic stuff driving you guys crazy right now, or is it manageable?"*
- **If they agree:** *"That's exactly why I'm calling Mark. When is the best time for me to catch him so I can show him how to get that off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script (Once Mark answers)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Warm Entry
> *"Hey Mark, it's Michael Anyanwu. We go to the same church. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then I'll let you go if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I run a local AI and automation consultancy here in DFW. I know you run two busy State Farm offices, and a massive problem I'm seeing with multi-location agencies is that the staff gets completely bogged down doing manual data entry in ECRM or answering repetitive billing questions, instead of actually writing new policies."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently balancing service calls versus sales across both locations?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When the Arlington office is slammed with service requests, what happens to a new quote request that comes in?"*
2. *"How much of Sam or Amanda's week is spent just answering repetitive billing or policy questions?"*
3. *"If you could instantly take 40% of the manual ECRM data entry off their plates, what would that mean for your new policy production?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"State Farm makes us use their software."** | *"That makes total sense. We don't replace ECRM—corporate would kill you. We build the 'glue' that intercepts the repetitive stuff before it even hits your staff. How well is your current system handling after-hours quote requests?"* |
| **"We are too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If your team is slammed, it means manual admin is eating into your sales time. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest operational bottleneck right now: perfectly syncing your two offices, or getting your team to do manual data entry?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how we automate that exact bottleneck without ripping out your current CRM. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
