# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Jake Thomas (Physical Therapy 365)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Jake Thomas, Founder & DPT
- **The Angle:** He runs a mobile PT clinic. He is either driving or doing manual therapy. He cannot answer the phone. His business line is his cell phone (which also receives texts).
- **The Entry:** Pure Cold Call. You will hit him directly. Highly likely to go to voicemail if he is in a session.

## 2. The Script (If Jake Answers)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Jake, it's Michael Anyanwu. I know I'm probably catching you in the car or with a patient — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's a waste of your time?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pre-Med Credibility)
> *"I'm a pre-med student here in DFW, but I run a business consultancy that builds automated systems for mobile clinicians. I was looking at Physical Therapy 365, and the biggest bottleneck I see with mobile PTs is that you're either driving or doing manual therapy, meaning your hands are full 8 hours a day. When a new lead calls to book an eval and you're in a session, they hit voicemail and often just call the next clinic on Google."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for capturing those inbound calls and patient intake forms while you're on the road?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"How much time do you spend sitting in your car returning voicemails and texting people intake forms before you can drive to the next appointment?"*
2. *"If you had a system that instantly texted back missed calls with a booking link and an automated digital intake waiver, how many more evaluations do you think you could fit into a week?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I just call them back when I get to my car."** | *"That’s the grind of mobile PT. But in the 45 minutes they were waiting for you to finish your session, how often have they already booked with a competitor who answered the phone?"* |
| **"We use an EMR that does that."** | *"Great, if your EMR is catching the missed calls and sending the two-way texts automatically, you're ahead of the curve. Does it actually get them to fill out the waiver before you arrive?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar mobile clinic automated their missed-call capture and intake. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*

---

## 6. VM/Text Strategy (If he doesn't answer)
Since his business line is advertised as a text number, if it goes to VM, do not leave a VM. Send this exact text:
> *"Hey Jake, Michael here. Tried to give you a quick ring but figured you were in a session doing manual therapy. I help mobile clinicians automate their missed-call text-backs and intake forms so you don't lose quotes while your hands are full. Got 5 mins later this week to chat?"*
