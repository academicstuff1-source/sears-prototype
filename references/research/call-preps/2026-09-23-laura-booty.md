# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Laura Booty (LB Health Insurance)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Laura Booty, Owner & Independent Broker
- **The Angle:** Open enrollment (OEP/AEP) is weeks away. She is about to be drowning in calls, document collection, and quotes. Every missed call is lost commission, but she can't answer the phone when she's actively enrolling another client.
- **The Entry:** Pure Cold Call. You are likely to hit her directly.

## 2. The Script (Once Laura answers)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Laura, it's Michael Anyanwu. I know I'm catching you right before the Open Enrollment madness begins — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's a waste of your time?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & The Q4 Pain Point)
> *"I run a business consultancy here in DFW that builds automated systems for solo operators. I know you're running LB Health Insurance, and with Medicare AEP and ACA enrollment just weeks away, the biggest bottleneck I see with independent brokers is that when you're on a 45-minute enrollment call, you physically can't answer the phone for a new lead. If they hit your voicemail, they just call the next broker on Google."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for capturing those inbound calls and automatically collecting their Scope of Appointment forms while you're tied up?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"How much time do you spend chasing down drug lists and doctor lists before you can even run a quote?"*
2. *"If you had a system that instantly texted back missed calls with a link to securely upload their drug list, how much time would that save you per week during November?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I just call them back when I'm done."** | *"That’s the reality of a solo agency. But during AEP, how often do you call them back an hour later only to find out they already spoke to someone else?"* |
| **"I don't need any new software."** | *"I completely get it, you probably have enough CRMs to deal with. I don't sell software, I just map out the workflows to catch the leads falling through the cracks. Are you currently able to instantly text a missed call to capture the lead?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar solo broker automated their missed-call capture and document collection for Q4. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
