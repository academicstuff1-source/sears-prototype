# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Practice Administrator at Texas Center for Facial Plastic Surgery

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Practice Administrator or Managing Director
- **The Angle:** They run a hybrid Surgical Practice and Med Spa. Because they sell $20k surgeries, their front desk naturally prioritizes high-ticket surgical leads. The $500 Botox or filler leads (the Med Spa side) end up sitting in the inbox for hours, leaking revenue.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with a few other hybrid surgical and med-spa practices in Texas. A massive problem they face is that their front desk is naturally focused on delivering white-glove service to high-ticket surgical patients. Because of that, the standard Med Spa inquiries—like Botox or filler leads from social media—end up sitting unread for hours, and they bleed out to local competitors."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently balancing the high-ticket surgical intake with the high-volume med spa inquiries without letting leads slip?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When an Instagram DM comes in asking for lip filler pricing, does your front desk have to stop dealing with a post-op surgical patient to answer it?"*
2. *"Are your local receptionists responsible for chasing down Med Spa leads manually while also trying to coordinate surgical schedules?"*
3. *"If you could completely automate the Med Spa booking side so your staff only talked to surgical patients, what would that do for your front desk's bandwidth?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We just have the front desk handle all of it."** | *"That makes total sense, most hybrid practices do. The problem usually hits when the front desk has to choose between a surgical consult standing in the lobby and a Med Spa lead on an iPad. Which one usually gets ignored? How confident are you that zero Med Spa leads are slipping through the cracks?"* |
| **"We use Nextech/Symplast/EHR."** | *"That’s great, those are incredibly secure clinical systems. We don’t replace those—we just build the automated 'glue' so inbound Med Spa leads are instantly triaged via SMS before a human has to log into Nextech to enter them. Are your current tools fully booking the Med Spa patient automatically?"* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual lead follow-up is likely eating your team's bandwidth. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar hybrid practice automated their inbound Med Spa triage so their front desk never had to answer a basic pricing question again. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your operational gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
