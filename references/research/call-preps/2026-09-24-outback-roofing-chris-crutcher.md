# 📞 Follow-Up Call Battlecard: Chris Crutcher (Outback Roofing)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Chris Crutcher, Owner
- **Company:** Outback Roofing & Restoration
- **Target Demo:** Established SMB Roofing. Navy Vet. Former President of RCAT (Roofing Contractors Association of Texas).
- **The Angle:** "The Sales Team Follow-Up." He is not a guy in a truck; he's a VIP operator. His bottleneck isn't getting leads—it's getting his sales reps to actually follow up and log data into their CRM (like JobNimbus) without him having to micromanage them.
- **Current Status:** Second attempt. You called yesterday (Sept 23) and hit his voicemail, but didn't leave a message. 

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
If you hit an office manager or dispatcher:
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Chris in the office, or is he out in the field right now?"*
- **The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with home service owners on automating their lead follow-ups. I imagine as the one managing the office, it gets pretty frustrating trying to chase down the sales reps to make sure they actually updated the CRM after a site visit?"*
- **The Ask for Access:** *(If they agree)* *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with him. What is the best way for me to catch him so I can show him how to take that CRM chasing completely off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Chris, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I know you're highly established in the Texas roofing scene. I work with local owners to solve the biggest bottleneck in the industry right now: getting your sales reps to actually follow up with leads perfectly, without you having to yell at them to update the CRM."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently handling those follow-ups when they leave a house without a signed contract?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When a rep finishes a roof inspection but the homeowner says 'we need to think about it', what is your exact system for making sure they get followed up with on Day 3 and Day 7?"*
2. *"How much of your week is spent just auditing the CRM to see who actually called their leads back?"*
3. *"If you could guarantee every unsold lead got a perfectly timed follow-up text without your reps having to do it manually, what would that do to your close rate?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We use JobNimbus / AccuLynx."** | *"Those are great for project management. We don't replace them—we build the automation around them so when a rep moves a deal to 'Thinking About It', the follow-up texts fire automatically without them lifting a finger. How is your team's compliance with updating the CRM right now?"* |
| **"My guys are good at following up."** | *"That’s rare and awesome. Usually, top closers are terrible at admin. If they are already following up, I build the systems that do it for them so they can spend 100% of their time on roofs closing deals. Would it be a terrible idea to see what that looks like?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest headache right now: getting inbound calls answered instantly, or getting reps to follow up on unsold quotes?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how an automated sales follow-up pipeline works for roofing. The worst that happens is you get a free map of the workflow to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
