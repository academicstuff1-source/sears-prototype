# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Wes (Rapid Relief Mobile IV)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Wes, Founder (Former Firefighter/Paramedic)
- **The Angle:** He's running a mobile IV service with his dad. When he is with a patient, he's maintaining a sterile field and cannot answer the phone. Missed calls equal lost high-ticket revenue.
- **The Entry:** Pure Cold Call. You are highly likely to hit Wes or John directly on their cell phone.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Wes, it's Michael Anyanwu. I know I'm probably catching you on the road or with a client — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's a waste of your time?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pre-Med Credibility)
> *"I'm a pre-med student here in DFW, but I run a business consultancy that builds automated systems for independent clinical operators. I know you and your dad are running Rapid Relief Mobile IV, and as a former paramedic, you know that when you're maintaining a sterile field or dropping a line, you can't answer your phone. The biggest problem I see with mobile IV clinics is losing a $200 booking just because they missed a call while with another patient."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for capturing those inbound calls and intake forms when your hands are full?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"How much of your evening is spent manually chasing down consent forms or replying to texts from people who wanted to book earlier today?"*
2. *"IV hydration is so time-sensitive—if someone calls with the flu or a hangover and you don't answer, do they leave a voicemail, or do they just call the next clinic?"*
3. *"If you had a system that instantly texted back missed calls with an automated booking link and digital consent form, how many more drips do you think you could fit into a week?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We just use an answering service / voicemail."** | *"That’s better than nothing, but in this market, people want instant gratification. Does your answering service actually get them to fill out their medical intake form, or does that still fall back on you?"* |
| **"We aren't looking to change our software."** | *"I completely get it, I don't sell software. I just map out patient workflows for cash-pay clinics. How smooth is your process right now for getting digital waivers signed before you show up to the house?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you a generic link, what's a bigger headache for you on the road: missing inbound booking calls, or managing the patient intake forms?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar mobile clinic automated their missed-call capture and patient waivers. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
