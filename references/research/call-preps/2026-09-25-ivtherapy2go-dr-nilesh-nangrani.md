# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Dr. Nilesh Nangrani at IVTherapy2Go

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Dr. Nilesh Nangrani, D.O., Founder
- **The Angle:** Mobile IV Therapy. A highly on-demand service where speed-to-lead is everything. If someone wants an IV for a hangover or migraine, they book the first place that answers. Missing calls or slow dispatch ruins conversions. Michael's pre-med background provides instant credibility here.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Dr. Nangrani, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I have a pre-med background and I work with independent healthcare operators in DFW. A massive problem mobile IV clinics face is speed-to-lead. When a patient is feeling awful and wants an IV, they call around until someone answers. Missing that call while nurses are on dispatch usually means losing the booking instantly to a competitor."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently capturing leads that come in when all your dispatch nurses are busy or after-hours?"*

## 3. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(CRITICAL RULE: Always verify identity first. Ask "Is this Dr. Nangrani?" Owner-operators often answer the phones themselves).*

**The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Dr. Nangrani around, or is he tied up with patients right now?"*
**The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with independent IV clinic owners to build automation systems. I imagine as the one managing dispatch, it gets incredibly chaotic for you fielding frantic IV requests and trying to manually track down nurses?"*
**The Ask for Access:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with him. What is the best way for me to catch Dr. Nangrani so I can show him how to take that dispatch bottleneck completely off your plate?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"When a lead calls at 7 AM on a Sunday and no one can pick up, what exactly happens? Do they leave a voicemail, or just book elsewhere?"*
2. *"How much of your dispatch coordination requires manual text messaging and scheduling back-and-forth?"*
3. *"If you did nothing about missed calls for the next 12 months, what does that actually cost you in lost cash-pay revenue?"*
4. *"What would it mean for your business if every missed call was instantly texted back with a self-service dispatch booking link?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We already have a booking portal."** | *"That makes total sense. We don't replace your portal—we build the 'glue' that automatically texts missed calls within 30 seconds and pushes them to that portal. How well is your current setup stopping lead leakage when you're busy?"* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means you're likely missing calls that could be high-ticket drips. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest bottleneck right now: missing incoming calls, or manual nurse dispatching?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a mobile clinic solved this exact missed-call issue. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on Tuesday?"*
