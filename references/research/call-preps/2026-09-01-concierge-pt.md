# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Operations Director / Owner at Concierge PT

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Operations Director / Owner
- **The Angle:** They are a mobile/in-home PT business. Field therapists have zero front-desk support when they are driving across DFW. If therapists are manually texting ETAs, managing schedule changes, or dealing with digital intake forms from the front seat of their car, it is burning them out and destroying efficiency.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey [Name], it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook
> *"I work with mobile and in-home healthcare businesses in DFW. Because your team is entirely in the field, a massive problem other mobile owners are facing is 'windshield admin'. Their therapists are burning time sitting in their cars manually texting patient ETAs, dealing with schedule gaps, and chasing intake forms instead of driving or treating."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your therapists are currently handling patient communication and ETAs while they're out on the road?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain)*
1. *"When a therapist is running 15 minutes late in DFW traffic, what is the exact process for getting the patient updated?"*
2. *"How much time per week do you think your average therapist spends doing administrative texting or emailing from their phone?"*
3. *"If you could completely automate appointment reminders and ETAs so your therapists never had to look at their phone while driving, how much time would that save your team?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"Our EMR / routing app does that."** | *"That makes total sense, we don't replace your EMR. But a lot of routing apps don't seamlessly text the patient if the therapist gets stuck in traffic. Are your therapists still having to manually text updates?"* |
| **"We are too busy."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means 'windshield admin' is eating into the volume of patients your team can see. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is a bigger bottleneck right now for your field team: communicating ETAs, or getting intake paperwork done before they arrive?"* |

## 5. The Close
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how another mobile healthcare business automated their field communications so therapists just drive and treat. Do you have 15 minutes on Thursday?"*
