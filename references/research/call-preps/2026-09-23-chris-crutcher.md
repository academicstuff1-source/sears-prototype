# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Chris Crutcher (Outback Roofing)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Chris Crutcher, Owner
- **The Angle:** He is a Navy Veteran and former President of the Texas Roofing Association. He is highly established. His problem isn't getting leads; it's getting his sales reps to properly follow up with them without dropping the ball.
- **The Entry:** Pure Cold Call. Given his status, you will likely hit an office gatekeeper.

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (The Influencer Pivot)

### The Setup
*Goal: You are calling a highly established owner. Sound like an industry peer.*
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Chris around, or is he out in the field today?"*
- **The Pivot (If he's out/busy):** *"No worries. To be honest, I'm working with a few local owners on a system that automates the lead follow-up so the office staff doesn't have to constantly chase down sales reps for updates. As the one managing the office, is getting the reps to actually use the CRM a headache for you guys right now?"*
- **If they agree:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to pick Chris's brain. When is the best time for me to catch him so I can show him how to automate that off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script (Once Chris answers)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Chris, it's Michael Anyanwu. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's a waste of your time?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I run a local business consultancy here in DFW. I know you've been in the game a long time and even ran RCAT, so you know this better than anyone: the biggest headache for an established roofing company is getting your sales reps to actually follow up with leads and log their notes without you having to constantly babysit them."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how you're currently guaranteeing that your reps are following up with every single inbound lead that hits the office?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When an inbound lead comes in and gets assigned to a rep, what happens if that homeowner doesn't answer the first phone call?"*
2. *"How much revenue do you think you leave on the table each storm season just from reps forgetting to send a follow-up text?"*
3. *"If you could put a system in place that automatically chased down the homeowner until they replied, without your reps having to lift a finger, what would that do for your margins?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We already use JobNimbus / AccuLynx."** | *"Those are great for project management, but they are notoriously clunky for front-end sales follow-up. We don't replace your CRM; we build the 'glue' that does the follow-up automatically before the lead even gets into JobNimbus."* |
| **"My guys are good at following up."** | *"That’s rare, and it's probably why you guys are so established. But if you doubled your lead volume tomorrow, would your current manual follow-up process break?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what's your biggest headache right now: missed inbound calls, or reps not following up on existing quotes?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar contractor put their sales follow-up on autopilot. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
