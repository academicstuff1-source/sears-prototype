# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Samuel Holmes (Nxt Steps Financial Group)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session before the Q4 rush.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Samuel Holmes, Co-founder / Insurance Agent
- **Company:** Nxt Steps Financial Group
- **Target Demo:** Independent Medicare Broker (DFW).
- **The Angle:** "The Triage Playbook." Just like Cheray Scott, he deals with Medicare beneficiaries. AEP starts Oct 15th. He is going to get buried in repetitive questions from loyal clients and manual intake. We are pitching the *blueprint* for a human receptionist or temp hire to triage calls effectively, keeping him out of the weeds so he can write new policies.
- **Current Status:** First touch. Pure cold call. 

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(If you hit an admin or virtual assistant)*
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Samuel around, or is he tied up on an enrollment call?"*
- **The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with independent brokers to build call triage playbooks for Open Enrollment. I imagine as the one managing the front, it’s about to get incredibly chaotic for you fielding all the repetitive Medicare questions and chasing down Scope of Appointment forms?"*
- **The Ask for Access:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with him before the Oct 15th rush. What is the best way for me to catch him so I can show him how to take that call triage completely off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Samuel, it's Michael. I know I'm an interruption in your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with independent Medicare brokers in DFW. With AEP less than a month away, a massive problem I see is that brokers get absolutely bogged down fielding repetitive questions from loyal clients, and they don't have a structured playbook to have someone else triage those calls while they write new business."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for triaging that call overflow during the AEP rush so you don't have to play receptionist?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"When the AEP rush hits, how many calls per week are just repetitive questions that someone else could handle if they had the right script?"*
2. *"Before you can even run a quote, you have to collect their doctor list, drug list, and a Scope of Appointment. How much of your week is spent personally chasing those down?"*
3. *"If you go into Open Enrollment without a triage system in place, how many hours a week will you personally lose to playing receptionist?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I just plan to hire a temp or an assistant."** | *"I completely understand. But whoever you hire needs a bulletproof script so your seniors feel taken care of. What I do is build the exact triage playbook that makes your next hire successful from day one. Would it make sense to build the blueprint first before you pay someone hourly?"* |
| **"I don't want AI talking to my seniors."** | *"I couldn't agree more. Your clients expect a human, white-glove experience. This isn't about AI voices—this is about giving your human team a bulletproof playbook so they can triage calls exactly the way you would."* |
| **"I am too busy right now preparing for Q4."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed now, October is going to be brutal. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week just to look at a workflow map before the rush hits?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a call triage pipeline works for independent brokers. The worst that happens is you get a free blueprint for your next hire to use. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
