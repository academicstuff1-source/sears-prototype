# 📞 Follow-Up Call Battlecard: Cheray Scott (CS InsureWise)

**Goal:** Secure a meeting to map out the manual call triage system/playbook for her Open Enrollment rush.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Cheray Scott, Owner of CS InsureWise
- **Target Demo:** Loyalty-driven seniors (Medicare age) who screen all calls.
- **The Pain:** Open enrollment is approaching. She has massive call overflow and no one to triage repetitive questions. She wants work-life balance but relies entirely on a white-glove, human concierge model.
- **The Angle:** Consulting-led play. We aren't selling AI (she is highly AI-averse). We are selling the *system/playbook* that she can hand off to her $25/hr VA or a new human receptionist to handle the overflow effectively.
- **Current Status:** Re-engaging after she heart-reacted a reframe text on Sept 18, and missed your Sept 22 check-in text.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: The Warm Re-engagement
> *"Hey Cheray, it's Michael Anyanwu. I'm following up on my text from Tuesday. Do you have two minutes, or are you in the middle of client prep?"*

### Phase 2: The Direct Hook (Open Enrollment Urgency)
> *"I'm calling because Open Enrollment is right around the corner, and I know from our last conversation that call overflow is a massive bottleneck for you. You mentioned you want more work-life balance, but you need someone to triage those repetitive questions with the same white-glove touch your seniors expect."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to map out exactly what that triage playbook looks like, so whoever you hire can handle the overflow flawlessly without you needing to step in?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If she engages, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When the Open Enrollment rush hits, how many calls per week are just repetitive questions that someone else could handle if they had the right script?"*
2. *"If you go into this Q4 without a triage system in place, how many hours a week will you personally lose to playing receptionist?"*
3. *"What is the exact process you want your VA (or a new hire) to follow when a senior client calls in, so they feel taken care of without taking up your time?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I just want to hire someone (you)."** | *"I completely understand. While I don't operate as an outsourced receptionist, what I *do* is build the exact system that makes your next hire successful from day one. Would it make sense to build the blueprint first before you pay someone hourly?"* |
| **"I don't want AI talking to my seniors."** | *"I couldn't agree more. Your clients expect a human, white-glove experience. This isn't about AI voices—this is about giving your human team a bulletproof playbook so they can triage calls exactly the way you would."* |
| **"We are too busy right now."** | *"That’s exactly why I called. The longer we wait, the deeper into Open Enrollment we get. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week just to outline the process?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to put together a simple, visual map of this call triage flow. It takes about 15 minutes. The worst that happens is you get a free blueprint for your next hire, and if you want my help building it out, we can discuss that. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
