# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: David Cuellar (Redfalcon Roofing)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** David Cuellar, Owner
- **The Angle:** He advertises being open 7 days a week, 6 AM to 10 PM. He is highly sales-driven (runs referral programs and waives deductibles). If he's personally overseeing jobs 16 hours a day, he is either missing calls or burning out.
- **The Entry:** Pure Cold Call. You may hit him directly on his cell or reach an office admin.

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (If you hit an Office Manager)

### The Influencer Pivot
*Goal: If they are open 6 AM to 10 PM, the person answering the phone is exhausted.*
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is David around, or is he out on a job site?"*
- **The Pivot (If he's out):** *"No worries. To be honest, I'm working with a few local roofing owners on a system that intercepts inbound quote requests after hours or when the team is in the field. Since you guys are open until 10 PM every day, I imagine you are the one getting slammed with calls when you're trying to eat dinner?"*
- **If they agree:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to pick David's brain. When is the best time for me to catch him so I can show him how to take those late-night follow-ups off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script (Once David answers)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey David, it's Michael Anyanwu. I know I'm catching you in the middle of a job — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's a waste of your time?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I run a local business consultancy here in DFW. I saw that Redfalcon is open 6 AM to 10 PM, 7 days a week. A massive problem I see with aggressive owner-operators is that you end up fielding quote requests at 9 PM on a Sunday while trying to manage crews all day, which usually leads to either missed leads or total burnout."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for capturing leads when your phone rings after hours or when you're up on a roof?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When a homeowner calls at 8:30 PM, what happens if you're eating dinner and can't answer?"*
2. *"I noticed you run a referral program—how much time do you spend manually tracking who referred who and following up with them?"*
3. *"If you could instantly engage a missed call via an automated text that grabs their address, what would that do for your close rate this season?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I always answer my phone, even at 10 PM."** | *"That’s exactly why you're growing. But does it ever feel like your business owns you, rather than you owning the business? What happens if you take a day off?"* |
| **"I'm not interested in buying software."** | *"I completely understand. I don't sell software subscriptions. I map out workflow bottlenecks for local owners. How well is your current setup handling follow-ups on your referral program?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what's your biggest headache right now: missing calls while on a roof, or tracking all your referrals?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar contractor automated their after-hours lead capture. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
