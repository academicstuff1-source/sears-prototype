# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Ben Gallegos at Pinnacle Mobile Therapy

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Ben Gallegos, Owner / PT
- **The Angle:** 100% mobile physical therapy practice in DFW. As an owner-operator on the road, missing calls while treating patients directly translates to lost revenue. Admin tasks like intake and scheduling eat into his evening/weekend personal time.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Ben, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with other independent PT owners in DFW. A massive problem mobile practitioners face is missing new patient calls while treating, or spending 2 hours every evening doing manual intake and CRM data entry. It's usually costing them new leads or burning them out on admin work."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your practice is currently handling new patient inquiries when you're on the road?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"When a lead calls while you're in a session, what exactly happens? Do they leave a voicemail, or do they just call the next clinic?"*
2. *"How much of your evening or weekend is spent just chasing people for missing intake paperwork or confirming appointments?"*
3. *"If you did nothing about this for the next 12 months, what does that actually cost you in lost cash-pay revenue?"*
4. *"What would it mean for your schedule if appointment booking and intake were completely automated in the next 30 days?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I already have an answering service/EHR."** | *"That makes total sense. We don't replace that—we build the 'glue' that automatically texts missed calls within 30 seconds so they don't call a competitor. How well is your current setup stopping lead leakage?"* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means admin is eating your margins and personal time. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest bottleneck right now: missing calls during treatments, or manual data entry at night?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a mobile clinic solved this exact issue. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on Tuesday?"*
