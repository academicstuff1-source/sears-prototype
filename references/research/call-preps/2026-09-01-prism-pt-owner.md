# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Founder / Owner at PRISM PT

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Founder / Owner
- **The Angle:** They run a cash-pay / out-of-network model focused on holistic, rapid independence. Cash-pay models require high-touch lead nurturing. If a website lead submits an inquiry after hours and isn't answered immediately, they likely bounce to an in-network competitor. Speed-to-lead is their biggest vulnerability.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey [Name], it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your clinical day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook
> *"I work with out-of-network and cash-pay private practices in DFW. Because you aren't relying on massive insurance networks to feed you patients, speed-to-lead is critical. A massive problem other cash-pay owners are facing right now is after-hours web inquiries—if a patient reaches out at 8 PM and doesn't hear back until the next morning, they've often already booked with an in-network competitor."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently handling instant follow-up for leads that come in when the clinic is closed?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain)*
1. *"When someone submits an inquiry on your site asking about pricing, what exactly is the sequence to get them on the phone?"*
2. *"How much time is your staff spending manually nurturing leads that hesitate because of the out-of-network cost?"*
3. *"If you could guarantee a 5-minute response time to every single inquiry, 24/7, how many more evaluations do you think you'd book a month?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We get back to people first thing in the morning."** | *"That makes total sense, and during business hours your team is probably lightning fast. But in cash-pay, the data shows if they don't get a response in 5 minutes, conversion drops 80%. Have you guys looked into automating just that initial after-hours touchpoint?"* |
| **"We use Jane App / PtEverywhere."** | *"Great systems. We don't replace them—we just build the 'glue' so that a web lead instantly triggers an SMS conversation before it ever hits the EMR. How is [System] handling instant 2-way texting for you right now?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is a bigger bottleneck right now: converting web leads into evaluations, or the actual patient intake paperwork?"* |

## 5. The Close
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how another out-of-network clinic automated their 24/7 lead response to stop losing patients to insurance mills. Do you have 15 minutes on Thursday?"*
