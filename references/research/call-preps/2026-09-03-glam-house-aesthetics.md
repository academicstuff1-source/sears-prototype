# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Founder / Clinic Manager at Glam House Aesthetics

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** The Founder (often the Lead Injector) or the Clinic Manager.
- **The Angle:** They are at a rapid growth inflection point (expanding in the San Antonio area). At this stage, the Founder is usually still the bottleneck—answering Instagram DMs at 9 PM on a Sunday because they don't have an automated system and they don't want to lose the revenue.

## 2. The Gatekeeper Bypass (Use this if a receptionist answers)
> *"Hey [Name], it's Michael. I was hoping you could help me out. Who handles the operational side of the clinic right now—is that the Clinic Manager or does the Owner still handle that?"*
> *(If they ask why you're calling):* *"I'm calling about how the clinic manages the after-hours Instagram leads, but I wasn't sure who specifically oversees that system. Is that [Name]?"*

## 3. The Script (Use this ONLY when you get the Decision Maker)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with a few other rapidly growing med-spas in Texas. A massive problem they face when they start expanding is that the Founder or Clinic Manager is still personally answering Instagram DMs and inquiries at 9 PM on a Sunday because they don't have an automated way to capture and qualify them after hours. It basically traps them in the business 24/7."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently handling those after-hours social media leads without sacrificing your weekends?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When a lead DMs you on Instagram at 8 PM asking for lip filler pricing, who is actually responding to that, and how quickly does it happen?"*
2. *"If you just turned your phone off all weekend, how much revenue do you think would slip through the cracks by Monday morning?"*
3. *"If you could completely automate the qualification of those leads so your front desk just walked into booked appointments on Monday, what would that do for your bandwidth?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I (the owner) just handle it on my phone."** | *"That makes total sense, most founders do when they are building the brand. But as you expand to more locations, you can't be awake 24/7. How close are you to hitting a wall where you physically can't answer them all fast enough?"* |
| **"We use Aesthetic Record / Boulevard."** | *"That’s great, those are excellent booking systems. We don’t replace those—we just build the automated 'glue' so inbound Instagram leads are instantly qualified via an AI text conversation before they even hit your booking system. Are your current tools doing two-way triage?"* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual lead follow-up is likely eating your personal time. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar founder automated their inbound social triage so they never had to answer a pricing DM on a Sunday again. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your operational gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
