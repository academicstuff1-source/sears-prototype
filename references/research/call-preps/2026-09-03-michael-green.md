# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Michael B. Green

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Michael B. Green, Independent Recruiter (Fortrea/Clinical Trials Ecosystem)
- **The Angle:** Independent recruiters live and die by their candidate pipeline. If he is independent, he doesn't have a team of sourcers. He is likely doing his own manual data entry—scraping LinkedIn profiles, moving candidates into an ATS, and tracking follow-ups. Every hour spent copy-pasting is an hour not spent closing a candidate. 

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Michael, it's Michael Anyanwu. We know each other through the Fortrea and clinical trials network. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I recently started an AI consulting business, and I've been working with independent recruiters and operators in our space. A massive problem they face is that their week gets completely swallowed by manual candidate data entry—scraping profiles, updating the ATS, and chasing follow-ups. It's usually costing them 10 to 15 hours a week of pure copy-pasting."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how much of your week is currently tied up doing that kind of manual pipeline management?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When you find a great candidate profile, what exactly are the manual steps you have to take before they are fully logged and engaged?"*
2. *"How much of your week is spent just chasing candidates for missing information or interview confirmations?"*
3. *"If you could instantly get 10 hours of your week back from manual data entry, what would you do with that time?"*
4. *"If you don't automate this and keep doing it manually, what does that actually cost you in lost placements over the next 12 months?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I already use an ATS/CRM."** | *"That makes total sense, you have to. But we don't replace your ATS—we build the 'glue' that automatically scrapes the candidate data and puts it into your ATS for you. Are you currently doing that data entry manually?"* |
| **"I don't have time for this."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual admin is eating your margins. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest operational bottleneck right now: sourcing candidates, or managing the data/follow-ups?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how we automate candidate data pipelines for folks in our space. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
