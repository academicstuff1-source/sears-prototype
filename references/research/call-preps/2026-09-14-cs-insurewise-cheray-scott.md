# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: A. Cheray Scott at CS InsureWise

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** A. Cheray Scott, Independent Insurance Broker (Medicare, Life, Health)
- **The Angle:** Independent brokers dealing with Medicare and Life get slammed with manual data entry, quote follow-ups, and tracking annual enrollment periods. Our hypothesis is that her lead intake and "think about it" follow-up processes are highly manual, causing dropped leads and wasted hours.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Cheray, it's Michael. I actually found your contact info in the church bulletin. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with other local business owners and independent professionals here in DFW. A massive problem they're facing right now—especially in services like insurance and Medicare—is getting bogged down with manual intake, quote follow-ups, and CRM data entry. It's usually costing them roughly 10-15 hours a week of pure admin, or causing them to lose follow-ups on every third lead that says 'let me think about it'."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently handling that manual follow-up volume?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When someone asks for a Medicare or Life quote online or over the phone, what exactly happens between that moment and them actually signing?"*
2. *"How much of your week is spent just chasing people down for missing information or following up on quotes you already sent?"*
3. *"If you did nothing about this for the next 12 months, especially going into the next enrollment period, what does that actually cost you in lost commissions or wasted time?"*
4. *"What would it mean for your business if that entire follow-up bottleneck was running smoothly behind the scenes, without you having to manually touch it?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I already use an agency CRM / software."** | *"That makes total sense. Most successful brokers use a specialized CRM. We don't replace that—we build the 'glue' that stops you from having to manually move data in and out of it. How well is your CRM handling those personalized follow-ups behind the scenes for folks who don't buy on day one?"* |
| **"I hate AI / I want to keep the human touch."** | *"I'm so glad you said that. In your business, trust is everything. The goal isn't to replace you with a robot—it's to handle the boring data entry in the background so you have MORE time to be face-to-face with your clients."* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual admin is eating your time and margins. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest operational bottleneck right now: initially collecting client info, or following up on quotes?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how we solve this exact follow-up issue for other independent owners. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
