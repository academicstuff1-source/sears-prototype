# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Brianna Curtis at Blue Heron Midwifery

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Brianna Curtis, LM, CPM (Owner / Solo-practitioner)
- **The Angle:** Solo-practitioner Midwife. She prides herself on concierge-level, highly personal care. The friction is that doing high-touch care means she has zero time for admin work, leading to delayed follow-ups with new inquiries or hours spent sending intake forms and answering FAQs.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Brianna, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with independent medical and wellness practitioners in DFW. A massive problem solo providers face is that offering concierge-level care leaves them zero time for admin. They usually end up spending their evenings manually responding to website inquiries, sending out intake forms, and answering the same FAQs over and over."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how much of your personal time is currently being eaten up by chasing new patient inquiries and onboarding paperwork?"*

## 3. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(CRITICAL RULE: Always verify identity first. Ask "Is this Brianna?" Solo practitioners usually answer their own phones).*

**The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Brianna around, or is she at a birth right now?"*
**The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with independent midwifery practices to build operational workflows. I imagine as the one managing her front desk, it gets incredibly chaotic for you fielding frantic new patient inquiries and chasing down intake forms when she's unavailable?"*
**The Ask for Access:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with her. What is the best way for me to catch Brianna so I can show her how to take that patient intake bottleneck completely off your plate?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"When an expecting mother reaches out through your website, how long does it typically take to get them scheduled for a consultation, and how manual is that process?"*
2. *"How much of your evening or weekend is spent just managing emails and CRM data entry instead of resting or being with family?"*
3. *"If you did nothing about this admin overhead for the next 12 months, what does that actually cost you in burnout or lost capacity?"*
4. *"What would it mean for your practice if every new inquiry was instantly nurtured, qualified, and sent booking links automatically?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I like handling inquiries personally."** | *"That makes total sense, especially for midwifery where trust is everything. We don't automate the relationship—we automate the friction. We can set it up so they instantly receive a personalized video from you and a calendar link. How are you currently ensuring people don't slip through the cracks when you're at a birth?"* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means admin is eating your personal time. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest bottleneck right now: responding to new inquiries, or managing intake forms?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a solo practice solved this exact admin issue. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on Tuesday?"*
