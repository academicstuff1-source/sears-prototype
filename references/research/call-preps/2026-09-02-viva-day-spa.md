# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Operations Director at Viva Day Spa + Med Spa

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Operations Director / Regional Manager
- **The Angle:** They are a hybrid Day Spa *and* Med Spa running 3+ locations in Texas. The dual-model means their front desk is drowning in fragmented intake workflows—balancing simple massage waivers with strict medical history forms for injectables.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with other high-volume clinic operators here in Texas. A massive problem they face when scaling to 3 or 4 locations is their front desk getting bogged down with fragmented paperwork—specifically, manually coordinating standard spa waivers versus strict medical intake forms. It's usually eating up hours of payroll every week or creating bottlenecks in the lobby."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your Austin and Dallas teams are currently handling that dual-intake volume?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When a client books a massage online, but then adds on Botox at the counter, what exactly happens behind the scenes to get their medical history cleared?"*
2. *"How much of your staff's week is spent just chasing people for missing pre-care paperwork or verifying credentials?"*
3. *"If a high-margin injectable client is due for a 90-day refill, is your front desk tracking that manually, or is it fully automated?"*
4. *"If you did nothing about this for the next 12 months as you keep expanding, what does that actually cost you in wasted payroll or lost cross-sells?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We already have Zenoti/Mindbody."** | *"That makes total sense. Most clinics at your scale use a heavy enterprise system. We don't replace that—we build the 'glue' that stops your staff from having to manually move data in and out of it. How well is your system handling the split between standard spa and medical intake?"* |
| **"We are too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual admin is eating your margins. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest operational bottleneck right now: managing the medical intake paperwork, or chasing down follow-ups?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar clinic automated their cross-location intake workflows. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your operational gaps to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
