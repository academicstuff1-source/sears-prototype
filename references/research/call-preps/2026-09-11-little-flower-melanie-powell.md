# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Melanie Powell at Little Flower Birth Services

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Melanie Powell (Owner, Doula)
- **Number:** 469-706-0868
- **The Angle:** Her website is highly optimized to drive traffic to a "Free Discovery Call." Solo-practitioners running this model usually hit a wall with manual scheduling, reminders, and following up with leads who don't close on the first call. 

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & The Warm Anchor
> *"Hey Melanie, this is Michael. We actually live in the same neighborhood and go to the same church. I saw your ad in the bulletin recently."*
> *(Pause for her to acknowledge the connection)*
> *"I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with other local solo-practitioners to streamline their operations. I was looking at your website, and you have an amazing setup driving moms to that Free Discovery Call.* 
> 
> *But usually, the massive problem business owners hit at this stage is that the intake flow — getting those calls booked, sending reminders, and manually following up with the ones who don't commit right away — turns into a major time sink that pulls you away from actual client care."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how you're currently handling that scheduling and follow-up pipeline?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If she opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When a mom fills out the discovery call form at 8 PM, what exactly happens between then and the moment you actually get on the phone with her?"*
2. *"If someone does a discovery call but says 'I need to think about it,' how much time do you spend manually chasing them down over the next two weeks?"*
3. *"If you didn't have to touch the scheduling or follow-up process at all, what would you do with those extra hours every week?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I use Calendly / Acuity already."** | *"That’s great, Calendly is a solid tool. We don't replace that—we build the 'glue' around it. Calendly books the time, but what happens when they no-show, or when they do show up but don't buy immediately? Are you automating that follow-up sequence, or still doing it by hand?"* |
| **"I don't have enough volume for that to be a problem yet."** | *"That makes total sense. Usually it's better to build the pipes before the flood hits. Would it be a terrible idea to just map out what an automated pipeline looks like so you have it ready when the volume does spike?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest operational bottleneck right now: getting discovery calls booked, or closing them afterward?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how an automated discovery-to-close pipeline works for a solo-practice. The worst that happens is you get a free map of a workflow to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
