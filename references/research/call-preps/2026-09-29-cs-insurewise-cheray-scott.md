# 📞 Call Prep Battlecard: Cheray Scott at CS InsureWise

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session or directly agree on the scope to build her Triage Playbook.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** A. Cheray Scott, Independent Medicare Broker
- **The Angle:** **The "Anti-AI Triage Playbook."** She hates AI and wants a white-glove human touch for her senior clients. She is approaching 60, wants work-life balance, and faces massive call overflow during the upcoming AEP (Annual Enrollment Period). She currently pays a VA ~$25/hr for CRM/newsletters. You are not selling AI; you are building the bulletproof triage system/playbook so her *human* VA can screen and handle the AEP rush without bothering her.

## 2. The Human Override (Situational Awareness)
*(CRITICAL: Before you pitch, listen to their environment. Are they driving, actively lost, yelling at kids, or dealing with an emergency? If yes, DO NOT PITCH).*
> *"Oh wow, it sounds like you are in the middle of chaos right now. I am going to hang up and let you get sorted. I'll try you back later. Good luck!"*

## 3. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(Note: As an owner-operator, Cheray will likely answer directly, but if the VA answers, use this).*
**The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Cheray around, or is she tied up right now?"*
**The Pivot:** *"No worries. I'm calling because I know AEP is about to hit, and as the one managing the CRM and front lines, it’s about to get incredibly chaotic for you fielding all those repetitive Medicare questions."*
**The Ask for Access:** *"I'm helping Cheray build a Triage Playbook specifically to take that overflow completely off both of your plates. What is the best way for me to catch her for 5 minutes?"*

## 4. The Script
### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Cheray, it's Michael. I saw I missed a call from you on Thursday and wanted to catch you before the October AEP rush completely takes over your schedule. Do you have two minutes?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Re-anchoring the Anti-AI Playbook)
> *"Last time we spoke, you made it crystal clear you want absolutely zero AI talking to your seniors, which I completely respect. But you also mentioned you want your work-life balance back, and AEP is going to destroy that if you're fielding every single call yourself."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea for us to sit down for 15 minutes and map out a human-led Triage Playbook? Something your current VA can use to screen calls, answer the repetitive questions, and only pass the high-value problems to you?"*

## 5. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If she opens up, widen the pain and focus on the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When AEP hits next month, how much of your week is going to be spent just repeating the same basic Medicare deadlines to callers?"*
2. *"If your VA could handle 80% of the incoming call volume perfectly using a script we build, how many hours would that give you back each week?"*
3. *"What happens if you try to muscle through this AEP exactly the same way you did last year?"*

## 6. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I just want to hire you to answer the phones."** | *"I'm flattered, but I'd be way too expensive as a receptionist. My value is building the exact playbook and system so you can hand it to a $20/hr assistant and know they'll handle it perfectly without you."* |
| **"My clients only want to talk to me."** | *"That makes total sense. They trust you. But they don't need you just to confirm a deadline or check a document status. We only offload the administrative noise, preserving you for the high-value advice. How many of your calls last week actually required your expertise?"* |
| **"I'm too busy getting ready for AEP."** | *"I completely understand. But if you're already slammed now, October is going to be brutal. Would it be a terrible idea to invest 15 minutes next week to build a system that buys back 10 hours a week during the rush?"* |

## 7. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"Let's do this: Give me 15 minutes to show you a visual map of how we can route and triage your AEP calls using your current VA—no AI voices involved. The worst that happens is you get a free workflow map to keep. Does [Day] at [Time] work?"*
