# 📞 Call Battlecard: Dominic Rodriguez (Dent Machine)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Dominic Rodriguez, Owner/Operator (Mobile Paintless Dent Repair)
- **The Angle:** He is a solo, mobile operator. His hands are physically busy all day fixing cars, which means missed calls and manual texting for photo estimates are his biggest bottlenecks.
- **The Entry:** Warm connection (same church). No gatekeeper expected.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Warm Entry
> *"Hey Dominic, it's Michael Anyanwu. We go to the same church. I know you're probably out in the field working on a car right now — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then I'll let you go if it's not a fit?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I run a local automation consultancy. I was looking at your mobile dent repair setup, and a massive problem I see with guys in your industry is that when your hands are full doing a repair, you can't easily answer the phone or text back quotes, which usually means losing jobs to the next guy on Google."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for capturing those leads when you're physically busy?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When someone wants a quote and you ask them to text a picture of the dent, how much of your evening is spent reviewing those and texting people back?"*
2. *"If you miss a call while working on a car, what are the odds that person actually leaves a voicemail versus just calling another shop?"*
3. *"What would it mean for your schedule if those inbound calls automatically got a text asking for the photo, without you having to touch your phone?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I just call them back when I'm done."** | *"That makes total sense, and a lot of guys do that. But how often do you call them back an hour later and they say, 'Oh, I already found someone else to do it?'"* |
| **"I'm too busy to set anything up right now."** | *"I completely understand, and the fact that you're slammed is exactly why I called. If you're that busy, your phone is probably a distraction. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email / text me your info."** | *"Happy to. Just so I don't send you a generic link, what's your biggest headache right now: missing calls while working, or chasing people down for photo estimates?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a simple 5-minute visual map of how you can put your estimate intake on autopilot. The worst that happens is you get a free map to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
