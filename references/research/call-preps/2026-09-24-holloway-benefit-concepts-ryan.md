# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Ryan Holloway (Holloway Benefit Concepts)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session before his Q4 group renewal rush starts.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Ryan Holloway, Founder
- **Company:** Holloway Benefit Concepts
- **Target Demo:** Independent Health Broker (Focus on Small Business / Group Benefits).
- **The Angle:** "The Q4 Group Renewal Crunch." Unlike Medicare brokers who deal with seniors, Ryan deals with HR managers and business owners. His Q4 pain is chasing down "census data" (spreadsheets of employee ages/dependents) and getting individual employees to fill out health questionnaires so he can quote the group. It is notoriously manual and email-heavy.
- **Current Status:** First touch. Pure cold call. 

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(If you hit an admin or account manager)*
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Ryan around, or is he tied up with a client right now?"*
- **The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with independent brokers to automate their group renewals. I imagine as the one managing the accounts, it’s about to get incredibly chaotic for you chasing down census data and employee applications from HR managers via email?"*
- **The Ask for Access:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with him before Q4 hits. What is the best way for me to catch him so I can show him how to take that document chasing completely off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Ryan, it's Michael. I know I'm an interruption in your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with independent health brokers in DFW. With Q4 renewals coming up, a massive problem I see for guys doing group benefits is the manual nightmare of chasing down census data and employee applications from HR managers just to run a quote."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for automating that data collection so you aren't playing email-tag with HR?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When an employer says they want a quote, how many emails back and forth does it take to get a clean census spreadsheet from them?"*
2. *"If a group has 20 employees and 3 of them won't fill out their health questionnaires, how much time does your team waste hunting down those 3 stragglers?"*
3. *"If you go into Q4 doing this manually, how many hours a week will your team lose to just chasing paperwork instead of advising clients?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We use Employee Navigator / Ease."** | *"Those are fantastic platforms for enrollment. But getting the employer to actually upload the data to get the initial quote is usually the bottleneck. We build the automation *before* it hits Navigator. Are your clients perfectly uploading their own data right now?"* |
| **"I have an account manager who handles that."** | *"That’s great you have coverage. But if your account manager is spending 10 hours a week chasing spreadsheets, that's 10 hours they aren't helping you renew accounts. Would it make sense to automate that busywork for them?"* |
| **"I am too busy right now preparing for Q4."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed now, October is going to be brutal. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week just to look at a workflow map before the rush hits?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how an automated census collection pipeline works for group brokers. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
