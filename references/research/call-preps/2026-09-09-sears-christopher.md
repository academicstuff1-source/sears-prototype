# Discovery Call Prep: Christopher Sears (Clinical Trial Recruitment)

**Goal:** Present the strategic shift to B2B recruitment infrastructure and secure a paid pilot engagement to build the compliant approval-gated pipeline.

---

## 1. The Context (Pre-Call Reframe)
- **Who:** Christopher Sears (Warm Contact)
- **Current Reality (From Sept 9 Email):**
  - **Sources:** He runs a scraper pulling from 35 websites (Top 3: Celerion, ICON, Parexel) AND he makes manual calls to CRO call centers, capturing voice memos.
  - **Output:** Highly structured, specific trial info (dates, compensation up to $9.6k, BMI/ages, washout periods, specific meds). 
  - **Monetization Goal:** Wants to charge consumers via Telegram + Stripe.
  - **Tech Stack:** Already uses a scraper and ChatGPT daily.
- **The Pivot:** We are shifting the angle from a "consumer Telegram automation tool" (high compliance risk, low revenue ceiling) to "B2B compliant participant recruitment infrastructure" (scalable, lucrative, defensible).
- **The Core Insight:** Since he already has the data supply (scraper) and uses AI (ChatGPT), his true bottleneck isn't getting data—it's scaling revenue. The consumer subscription ceiling is low. The real value is an AI-accelerated internal pipeline that secures official authorization and routes consented referrals back to those 35 sites for a B2B fee.

## 2. The Meeting Outline

### Phase 1: The Reframe & Insight
> *"Chris, I spent a lot of time digging into the legal and operational side of clinical trial recruitment since we last spoke. I realized something massive: the most valuable business here isn't just 'posting hidden trials' to a Telegram group. The real business is building a compliant, opt-in participant-referral channel for these research sites."*

> *"Would you be opposed to looking at a slightly different workflow I mapped out that targets the B2B revenue side and completely eliminates the IRB compliance risk?"*

### The B2B Monetization Play (How Chris Makes Money)
If Chris asks how this model actually generates revenue, outline the progression:
1. **Fee per Consented Referral:** Research sites pay for verified, interested leads. We structure the workflow so prospective participants explicitly opt-in to be contacted by the study team.
2. **Campaign Setup Fees:** Charge CROs/Sites a flat fee to format their approved materials, distribute them to the segmented Telegram community, and set up the reporting loop.
3. **Monthly Retainer:** Once he proves he can drive quality referrals consistently, he shifts sites to a retainer for ongoing access to his audience and campaign management.
*(Key takeaway for Chris: You don't charge the patients; you charge the multi-million dollar research sites that are desperate for qualified participants.)*

### Phase 2: High-Yield Diagnostic Questions
*(Uncovering the current workflow friction and compliance gaps)*
1. *"Between the 35 sites you scrape and the manual calls you make, what is the exact manual process to clean up all that fragmented data and get it into Telegram?"*
2. *"How are you currently securing written authorization from these sites to ensure the posts aren't accidentally violating IRB recruitment advertising rules?"*
3. *"How much time does it take you to format everything with ChatGPT, and how many more opportunities could you process a week if that administrative work was zeroed out?"*
4. *"If we could offer sites a 'fee per consented referral' model instead of just charging your Telegram users, how would that change the ceiling of this business?"*

### Phase 3: The Pitch & Solution (The Compliant Pipeline)
> *"Here is what I propose we build. Instead of auto-publishing to Telegram—which is a legal landmine—we build an AI-accelerated approval pipeline."*

*(Walk through the safe architecture)*
`Calls/Voice Memos + Scraper (35 sites) -> Unified AI Data Extraction -> Internal CRM Record -> Automated Approval-Request Email -> Written Approval -> AI-Drafted Telegram Post -> Human Clicks Publish`

> *"The AI does 90% of the heavy lifting (transcribing calls, structuring the scraped data, drafting approval emails, formatting the Telegram posts), but you retain 100% control over compliance. I can build this exact pipeline for you as a 30-day pilot. Is that something you'd want to move forward with?"*

## 3. Objection Handling

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I just wanted a simple bot."** | *"I completely understand, and I can build that, but auto-publishing eligibility criteria is legally classified as recruitment advertising. If we don't put a human approval step in the middle, you risk getting blacklisted by the sites you're trying to help. This pipeline protects you."* |
| **"Will this slow down posting?"** | *"It actually speeds up your internal work. You just leave a voice memo, and the system tees up the approval email and the drafted post. You're just clicking 'approve' instead of typing everything out."* |
| **"I'm not ready to charge sites yet."** | *"That makes total sense. We can use this system just to organize your Telegram workflow right now, but it builds the foundation so you *can* charge them when you're ready. The data will be perfectly structured."* |
