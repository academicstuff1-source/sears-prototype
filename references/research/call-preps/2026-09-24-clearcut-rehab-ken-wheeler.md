# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Ken Wheeler (ClearCut Rehab)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Ken Wheeler, Founder & PT
- **Company:** ClearCut Rehab (Fort Worth)
- **Target Demo:** Cash-based, boutique, 1-on-1 Physical Therapy. 
- **The Angle:** "The Overwhelmed Owner-Operator." He runs a premium, high-touch clinic. This means two things: 1) Every missed call is a massive lost cash opportunity because he is likely in a session doing hands-on work, and 2) Late cancellations kill his margins if his front desk has to play phone tag to backfill the slot.
- **Current Status:** First touch. Pure cold call. 

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(If you hit an admin)*
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Ken around, or is he in a session right now?"*
- **The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with a few boutique clinics in the area to automate their waitlist backfill and missed calls. I imagine as the one managing the front, it gets incredibly frustrating when a patient cancels last minute and you have to play phone tag to fill the slot while the phones are ringing?"*
- **The Ask for Access:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with him. What is the best way for me to catch him between sessions so I can show him how to take that manual waitlist chasing completely off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Ken, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of a busy clinic day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with other premium, 1-on-1 clinic owners in DFW. A massive problem they're facing right now is that because they are so hands-on, their front desk gets bogged down playing phone tag to backfill last-minute cancellations, and missed calls go straight to voicemail. It ends up costing them thousands a month in empty slots and lost cash leads."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for instantly backfilling those cancellations without your staff having to manually call down a waitlist?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When a patient texts in a cancellation at 8 AM for a 10 AM slot, how much time does your team spend manually texting or calling people to fill it?"*
2. *"If a new premium lead calls while your front desk is busy, what exactly happens? Do they get an instant text, or do they hit voicemail and call a competitor?"*
3. *"If you go into Q4 without an automated backfill system, what does a single empty slot cost you?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We use JaneApp / WebPT and it handles our waitlist."** | *"JaneApp is fantastic for EMR, but it's notoriously weak at instantly texting 10 people on a waitlist and automatically booking the first one who replies. Are you guys currently doing that manually?"* |
| **"We rarely have cancellations."** | *"That’s incredible retention. What about new patient intake? Are you still having patients fill out manual PDFs, or is your onboarding 100% digital and automated before they walk in?"* |
| **"I am too busy to talk right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual admin is eating your margins. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a competitor solved this exact waitlist and missed-call issue. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
