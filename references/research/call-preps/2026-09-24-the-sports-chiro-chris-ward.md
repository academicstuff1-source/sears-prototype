# 📞 Follow-Up Call Battlecard: Dr. Chris Ward (The Sports Chiro)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Dr. Chris Ward, Owner / Sports Chiropractor
- **Company:** The Sports Chiro
- **Target Demo:** Cash-based Sports Chiropractor. Solo owner-operator.
- **The Angle:** "The Overwhelmed Owner-Operator." In cash-pay sports chiro, he is likely doing full 30-60 minute adjustments and soft tissue work. When his hands are on a patient, his phone rings to voicemail. Every missed call is a lost $150+ cash session.
- **Current Status:** Second attempt. You called yesterday (Sept 23), hit his voicemail (which confirmed he was in session), and sent a follow-up text. No reply yet.

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(If he happens to have a front desk admin answering today)*
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Dr. Ward around, or is he in a session with a patient right now?"*
- **The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with a few local cash-based clinics on automating their patient intake and missed calls. As the one managing the front, I imagine it gets pretty chaotic trying to chase down intake forms and catch every ringing phone while he is tied up in a session?"*
- **The Ask for Access:** *(If they agree)* *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with him. What is the best way for me to catch him between sessions so I can show him how to take that intake paperwork completely off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt (The "Self-Aware Follow-Up")
> *"Hey Chris, it's Michael Anyanwu. I sent you a text yesterday, but I know as a solo chiro you're probably elbow-deep in a patient session right now. Do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with other cash-based clinic owners in DFW. A massive problem they're facing right now is that because they actually spend a full hour hands-on with a patient, they physically cannot answer the phone. When a new lead calls while they're doing soft tissue work, that lead hits voicemail and often just calls the next clinic on Google. It's usually costing them thousands a month in missed cash revenue."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for instantly capturing those missed calls when your hands are full?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If he opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When a new athlete inquiry calls at 8 PM, what exactly happens between then and 8 AM the next day?"*
2. *"How much of your week is spent just chasing people for missing intake paperwork or confirmations instead of treating patients?"*
3. *"Cash-pay patients tend to drop off when they feel 'good enough'. What is your system for automatically following up with them 3 weeks later to get them back on the schedule?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We use JaneApp / WebPT for that."** | *"JaneApp is fantastic for EMR, but it's notoriously weak at speed-to-lead and two-way texting for missed calls. Are you currently able to instantly text a missed call to capture the lead before they dial a competitor?"* |
| **"I am too busy to talk right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual admin is eating your margins. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"I already have a front desk admin."** | *"That’s great you have coverage. But if your admin is on the phone with one patient, and another calls, does that second call get an instant text back with a booking link?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a competitor solved this exact missed-call issue. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep, and if it makes sense, we can talk about how to implement it. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
