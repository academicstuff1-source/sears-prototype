# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Director of Marketing at It's a Secret Med Spa

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Director of Marketing or VP of Operations
- **The Angle:** They run a highly aggressive, visual social media marketing strategy across DFW and Houston. When a brand scales marketing that fast across multiple locations, the local front desks usually can't keep up with the inbound DMs/texts, leading to massive "speed-to-lead" failure and wasted ad spend.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey, it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with a few other high-growth aesthetic clinics here in Texas. A massive problem they face when scaling their Instagram and ad spend across multiple locations is that their local front desks can't keep up with the inbound DMs and weekend inquiries. They end up bleeding high-intent leads because nobody is triaging them instantly, which basically sets fire to their marketing budget."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently ensuring every single Instagram DM or after-hours inquiry gets booked before they bounce to a competitor?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"When a lead DMs you at 9 PM on a Friday asking for lip filler pricing, what exactly happens between then and Monday morning?"*
2. *"Are your local receptionists in DFW and Houston responsible for chasing down these social leads manually while also trying to check in physical patients?"*
3. *"If you are spending [X] on ads to generate these inquiries, what does a 4-hour delay in response time actually cost you in lost bookings every month?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We just have the front desk handle it."** | *"That makes total sense, most clinics start out doing exactly that. The problem usually hits when the front desk has to choose between a physical patient standing in the lobby and a DM on an iPad. Which one usually gets ignored? How confident are you that zero leads are slipping through the cracks?"* |
| **"We already use Sprout/ManyChat/Zenoti."** | *"That’s great, you already have the infrastructure. We don’t replace those—we just build the automated 'glue' so those tools actually talk to your CRM and qualify the lead without a human having to type anything. Are your current tools fully booking the patient, or just sending automated greeting messages?"* |
| **"I'm too busy right now."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual lead follow-up is likely eating your team's bandwidth. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar multi-location clinic automated their inbound social triage so their front desk never had to answer a basic pricing DM again. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your operational gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
