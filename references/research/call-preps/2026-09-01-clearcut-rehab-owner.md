# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Owner / Clinical Director at ClearCut Rehab

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Owner or Clinical Director
- **The Angle:** As a boutique, 1-on-1 clinic, late cancellations absolutely destroy their margins. The front desk is likely wasting hours playing phone tag trying to manually backfill those gaps from a waitlist.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey [Name], it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your clinical day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook
> *"I work with private practice owners in DFW, specifically those running 1-on-1 models like ClearCut. A massive problem they're facing right now is margin compression from late cancellations. Their front desk is burning hours playing phone tag trying to manually backfill those slots from a waitlist."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently handling late cancellations and waitlist management?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain)*
1. *"When a patient cancels at 8 AM for a 2 PM slot, what exactly happens at the front desk to get that hour filled?"*
2. *"How much of your staff's week is spent just manually calling or texting the waitlist?"*
3. *"Because you offer a premium, 1-on-1 experience, do you feel like having patients fill out manual intake paperwork takes away from that 'white-glove' feel?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We already have WebPT / Jane App."** | *"That makes total sense. Most clinics I speak with use great EHRs. We don't replace that—we build the 'glue' outside of it so your staff doesn't have to manually trigger those waitlist texts. How well is [System] handling your last-minute backfills automatically?"* |
| **"We are too busy."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means manual admin like chasing cancellations is eating your margins. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest operational bottleneck right now: manual patient intake, or filling last-minute cancellations?"* |

## 5. The Close
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how another independent clinic automated their waitlist to fill cancellations in under 10 minutes. If it makes sense, great. If not, you can literally steal the strategy. Do you have 15 minutes on Thursday?"*
