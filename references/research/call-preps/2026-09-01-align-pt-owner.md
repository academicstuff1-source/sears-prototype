# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Owner / Practice Manager at Align PT

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Owner or Practice Manager
- **The Angle:** Because they specialize in complex care (Schroth Method, Pelvic Health), they live and die by physician referrals. When a fax or email referral comes in, the front desk has to manually chase the patient to get them booked. That "chase" is a huge drain on staff bandwidth and causes referral leakage.

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey [Name], it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook
> *"I work with specialized private practices in Dallas. Because clinics like yours handle complex care like pelvic health and scoliosis, you deal with a ton of physician referrals. A massive problem other owners are facing right now is the manual 'chase'—their front desk is burning hours every week playing phone tag with those referred patients trying to get them scheduled and intaked."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently tracking and following up on those inbound physician referrals?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain)*
1. *"When a referral comes in via fax or email, what exactly happens next to get that patient on the schedule?"*
2. *"How many times is your front desk having to call a referred patient before giving up?"*
3. *"Because your therapies are so specialized, getting their prior medical history must be a heavy lift. Are you guys still having them fill that out manually when they walk in?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We already have a system / EHR for that."** | *"That makes total sense. We don't replace your EHR. But most EHRs don't automatically text or email a patient the second a fax referral comes in. How much manual follow-up is your staff still doing?"* |
| **"We are too busy."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed, it means chasing referrals manually is eating your margins. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week when things cool down?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is a bigger bottleneck right now: following up on physician referrals, or the actual patient intake process?"* |

## 5. The Close
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how another specialized clinic automated their referral pipeline so the system chases the patient, not the front desk. Do you have 15 minutes on Thursday?"*
