# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Founder / Owner at PhysioLogix Texas

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Founder / Owner
- **The Angle:** They do in-home PT for older adults. That means they don't just communicate with the patient; they have to coordinate with the patient's adult children or caregivers. Keeping families in the loop about ETAs, schedules, and progress manually requires double the communication workload. 

## 2. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey [Name], it's Michael. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook
> *"I work with concierge healthcare practices in DFW. Because you focus specifically on in-home care for older adults, a massive problem other owners in that niche are facing is caregiver coordination. Their therapists and staff are burning hours manually calling or texting adult children to let them know when a therapist is arriving or to update them on scheduling changes."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask how your team is currently keeping patients' families in the loop without doubling your administrative workload?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If they open up, ask these to widen the pain)*
1. *"When a therapist is on their way to a senior's home, what is the exact process for ensuring both the patient and their family member know they are arriving?"*
2. *"How often do digital intake forms fail with your older demographic, forcing your staff to do manual phone intake?"*
3. *"If you could instantly automate schedule updates to go directly to a patient's adult child, how much phone time would that save your team a week?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"Our EMR handles our scheduling."** | *"That makes total sense, and we don't replace EMRs. But most EMRs are designed to message one patient, not run dual communication flows to a patient and a caregiver simultaneously. How much manual texting is your team still doing to keep families updated?"* |
| **"We prefer the personal touch of calling."** | *"I completely agree, personal touch is why they hire you. But calling to say 'we're 10 minutes away' isn't care, it's logistics. Automating the logistics gives you more time for the actual care. How are you handling ETAs right now?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is a bigger bottleneck right now: coordinating with caregivers, or just doing intake for older adults?"* |

## 5. The Close
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how another senior-focused clinic automated their caregiver updates so their staff stopped playing phone tag with families. Do you have 15 minutes on Thursday?"*
