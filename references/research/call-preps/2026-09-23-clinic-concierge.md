# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Lauren or Shayla (Clinic Concierge)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Lauren Payne or Shayla Dunlap (Co-Founders)
- **The Angle:** They run a direct-pay concierge clinic. High-touch care means high administrative burden. If they are with a patient, they can't field a call for a new sick visit. 
- **The Entry:** Pure Cold Call. You are likely to hit one of the founders directly, or possibly a single admin.

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (If you hit an Admin)
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Are Lauren or Shayla around, or are they out with patients right now?"*
- **The Pivot (If out):** *"No worries. I'm working with a few local direct-pay clinics on automating their patient intake and missed calls. As the person holding down the fort, I imagine it gets pretty chaotic trying to chase down consent forms and route the founders when they are on the road?"*
- **If they agree:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with them. When is the best time for me to catch them so I can show them how to take that intake paperwork off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script (Once a Founder answers)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey [Name], it's Michael Anyanwu. I know I'm probably catching you between patient visits — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's a waste of your time?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pre-Med Credibility)
> *"I'm a pre-med student here in DFW, but I run a business consultancy that builds automated systems for direct-pay clinics. I know you and Shayla are running Clinic Concierge, and with a mobile model, the biggest bottleneck I see is that when you're physically with a patient, you can't answer the phone for a new booking. If someone calls for a sick visit and you miss it, they just end up going to a local urgent care."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for capturing those inbound calls and intake forms when your hands are full?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"How much of your evening is spent manually chasing down consent forms or replying to texts from people who wanted to book earlier today?"*
2. *"If you had a system that instantly texted back missed calls with an automated booking link and digital consent form, how many more sick visits do you think you could capture a week?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We use an answering service / We have an admin."** | *"That’s great that you have coverage. But does that service actually get the patient to fill out their medical intake form, or does that still require manual follow-up from you or your admin?"* |
| **"We aren't looking for new software."** | *"I completely get it, I don't sell software. I just map out patient workflows for cash-pay clinics. How smooth is your process right now for getting digital waivers signed before you show up to a house?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar concierge clinic automated their missed-call capture and patient waivers. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
