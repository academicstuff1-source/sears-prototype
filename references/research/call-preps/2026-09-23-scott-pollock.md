# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Scott Pollock (Greater Than Performance)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Scott Pollock, Founder & Physical Therapist
- **The Angle:** He runs a cash-based PT clinic. He spends 60 minutes hands-on with patients, meaning his phone goes unanswered for hours a day. Every missed call is lost cash revenue.
- **The Entry:** Pure Cold Call. You are likely to hit Scott directly, or possibly a single front-desk admin.

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (If you hit an Admin)
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Scott around, or is he in a session with a patient right now?"*
- **The Pivot (If in session):** *"No worries. I'm a pre-med student working with a few local cash-based clinics on automating their patient intake and missed calls. As the one managing the front, I imagine it gets pretty chaotic trying to chase down intake forms and catch every call when Scott is tied up?"*
- **If they agree:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with him. When is the best time for me to catch him between sessions so I can show him how to take that intake paperwork off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script (Once Scott answers)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Scott, it's Michael Anyanwu. I know I'm probably catching you right between patient sessions — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's a waste of your time?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pre-Med Credibility)
> *"I'm a pre-med student here in DFW, but I run a business consultancy that builds automated systems for cash-based clinics. I know you're running Greater Than Performance, and the biggest bottleneck I see with cash-pay PTs is that because you actually spend a full hour hands-on with a patient, you physically cannot answer the phone. If a new lead calls while you're doing soft tissue work, they hit voicemail and often just call the next clinic on Google."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for capturing those inbound calls and intake forms when your hands are full?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"How much time do you or your admin spend chasing down new patients to make sure they filled out their intake forms before they show up?"*
2. *"If a patient drops off after 4 sessions because they feel 'good enough', what is your system for automatically following up with them 3 weeks later to get them back on the schedule?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"We use JaneApp / WebPT and it does everything."** | *"JaneApp is fantastic for EMR, but it's notoriously weak at speed-to-lead and two-way texting for missed calls. Are you guys currently able to instantly text a missed call to capture the lead?"* |
| **"I have an admin who answers the phone."** | *"That’s great that you have front desk coverage. But if your admin is on the phone with one patient, and another calls, does that second call get an instant text back with a booking link?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar cash-based clinic automated their missed-call capture and patient reactivation. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
