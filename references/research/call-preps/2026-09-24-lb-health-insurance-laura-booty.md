# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Laura Booty (LB Health Insurance Solutions)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session before her Q4 rush starts.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Laura Booty, Owner / Independent Health & Medicare Broker
- **Company:** LB Health Insurance Solutions
- **Target Demo:** Solo Operator. Medicare AEP (Oct 15) and ACA Open Enrollment (Nov 1) are right around the corner.
- **The Angle:** "The Q4 Bottleneck." She is about to enter her busiest season of the year. If she is on a 45-minute enrollment call with a senior citizen, she physically cannot answer her phone. Every missed call during Open Enrollment is lost commission.
- **Current Status:** First touch. Pure cold call. 

## 2. Gatekeeper Strategy (Turning them into an Influencer)
*(If you hit an admin or virtual assistant)*
- **The Ask:** *"Hey there, this is Michael. Is Laura around, or is she tied up on an enrollment call?"*
- **The Pivot:** *"No worries. I work with independent brokers to automate their inbound leads during Open Enrollment. I imagine as the one managing the front, it’s about to get incredibly chaotic for you chasing down Scope of Appointment forms and catching every call?"*
- **The Ask for Access:** *"That's exactly why I wanted to connect with her before the Oct 15th rush. What is the best way for me to catch her so I can show her how to take that document chasing off your plate?"*

## 3. The Script

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Laura, it's Michael. I know I'm an interruption in your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's not relevant?"*

### Phase 2: The Peer Hook (Specificity & Pain)
> *"I work with independent health brokers in the area. With Medicare AEP less than a month away, a massive problem solo brokers face is that when they are on a 45-minute enrollment call, they physically cannot answer the phone. If a new lead calls to get a quote and hits voicemail, they usually just call the next broker on Google."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for instantly capturing those missed calls when your hands are tied up during the Q4 rush?"*

## 4. High-Yield Discovery Questions
*(If she opens up, ask these to widen the pain and surface the cost of inaction)*
1. *"Before you can even run a quote, you have to collect their doctor list, drug list, and a Scope of Appointment. How much of your week is spent manually chasing those down via text or email?"*
2. *"If a client drops off or says 'let me think about it', what is your system for automatically following up with them so they don't forget to enroll by the deadline?"*
3. *"If you go into Open Enrollment without a system to capture missed calls instantly, how many commissions do you think slip through the cracks?"*

## 5. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I already use a CRM / Medicare platform."** | *"That makes total sense; you need a system like that for quoting. But we don't replace that—we build the automation around it so that when you miss a call, the system instantly texts them a link to upload their drug list before you even hang up your other call. Is your current CRM doing that instantly?"* |
| **"I am too busy right now preparing for Q4."** | *"I completely understand, and that’s exactly why I called. If you're slammed now, October is going to be brutal. Would it be a terrible idea to put 15 minutes on the calendar next week just to look at a workflow map before the rush hits?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | *"Happy to. So I don't send you generic spam, what is your biggest bottleneck heading into October: catching missed calls, or chasing down Scope of Appointment forms?"* |

## 6. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how an automated missed-call and intake pipeline works for independent brokers. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow gaps to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*
