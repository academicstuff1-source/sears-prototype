# 📞 Cold Call Battlecard: Dr. Chris Ward (The Sports Chiro)

**Goal:** Secure a 15-minute workflow mapping session.

---

## 1. The Context (10-Second Review)
- **Who:** Dr. Chris Ward, Founder & Chiropractor
- **The Angle:** He runs a cash-based sports chiropractic clinic. He does long, one-on-one sessions, meaning his phone goes unanswered for hours a day. Every missed call is lost cash revenue.
- **The Entry:** Pure Cold Call. You will likely hit him directly, or go straight to voicemail.

## 2. The Script (Once Dr. Ward answers)

### Phase 1: Pattern Interrupt & Permission
> *"Hey Dr. Ward, it's Michael Anyanwu. I know I'm probably catching you right between patient sessions — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up if it's a waste of your time?"*

### Phase 2: The Hook (Specificity & Pre-Med Credibility)
> *"I'm a pre-med student here in DFW, but I run a business consultancy that builds automated systems for cash-based sports clinics. I know you're running The Sports Chiro, and the biggest bottleneck I see with cash-pay docs is that because you do long, one-on-one sessions, you physically cannot answer the phone. If a new athlete calls while you're doing soft tissue work, they hit voicemail and often just call the in-network guy down the street."*

### Phase 3: The Pivot (No-Oriented Question)
> *"Would it be a ridiculous idea to ask what your system is right now for capturing those inbound calls and sports intake forms when your hands are full?"*

## 3. High-Yield Discovery Questions
1. *"How much time do you spend chasing down new patients to make sure they filled out their injury history before they show up?"*
2. *"When an athlete's pain goes away and they drop off the schedule, what is your system for automatically following up with them 4 weeks later for a maintenance session?"*

## 4. Objection Handling (Acknowledge & Pivot)

| Objection | The Play |
|---|---|
| **"I just call them back when my session ends."** | *"That’s the reality of a solo practice. But in the 40 minutes they were waiting for you to finish your session, how often have they already booked with a clinic that answered the phone?"* |
| **"We use JaneApp / WebPT and it does everything."** | *"JaneApp is fantastic for EMR, but it's notoriously weak at speed-to-lead and two-way texting for missed calls. Are you currently able to instantly text a missed call to capture the lead before they hang up?"* |

## 5. The Close (Zero-Risk Diagnostic)
> *"I’d love to show you a 5-minute visual map of how a similar cash-based clinic automated their missed-call capture and patient reactivation. The worst that happens is you get a free map of your workflow to keep. Do you have 15 minutes on [Day]?"*

---

## 6. VM/Text Strategy (If he doesn't answer)
If it goes to voicemail, DO NOT leave a VM. Text him this instead:
> *"Hey Dr. Ward, Michael here. Tried to give you a quick ring but figured you were in a session doing manual therapy. I help cash-based clinics automate their missed-call text-backs and intake forms so you don't lose quotes while your hands are full. Got 5 mins later this week to chat?"*
