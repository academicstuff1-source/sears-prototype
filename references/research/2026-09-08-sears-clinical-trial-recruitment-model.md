# Research Brief: Clinical Trial Recruitment Business Model (Christopher Sears)

> **Source:** Perplexity Pro
> **Date:** 2026-09-08
> **Context:** Pre-call prep for Christopher Sears discovery meeting (Sept 10, 2026)

---

## Business Overview

Christopher Sears operates a **clinical trial opportunity discovery business.** He calls official participant-facing call centers associated with CROs and research sites, learns about currently recruiting studies, organizes the information, and shares opportunities with a private Telegram community of people interested in participating in paid clinical research.

He calls **official participant-facing recruitment/prescreening lines** — not generic corporate receptionists. The call-center representatives are tasked with describing available studies, explaining basic requirements, discussing compensation, and making preliminary qualification assessments. This makes the calls a **more legitimate source of current operational study information** than an outdated public CRO webpage.

---

## Core Business Insight

The valuable business is not "posting hidden trials." The durable business is:

> **A compliant, fast, opt-in participant-acquisition and referral channel that connects interested people to authorized research-study opportunities.**

**Better positioning:**
> "We operate an opt-in participant community and help authorized research sites and recruitment partners distribute approved study opportunities, generate consented referrals, and route prospective participants to official screening teams."

**Avoid this language (legal and reputational risk):**
- "Hidden trials"
- "Secret study inventory"
- "Unpublished CRO trials"
- "Exclusive access"
- "We can get you enrolled"
- "We know you qualify"

---

## Key Compliance Distinction

Being allowed to *hear* study information as a prospective participant does **not** automatically give Christopher permission to publicly republish, rewrite, commercialize, or distribute that information to a Telegram audience.

Telephone recruitment and prescreening scripts are typically part of a study's recruitment process and are commonly subject to **IRB oversight.** An AI-generated Telegram post containing eligibility criteria, compensation, and a CTA should be treated as **recruitment advertising**, not a neutral directory entry.

**Main rule:**
> No written authorization and no approved material = no study-specific public post.

## Recommended Revenue Model (B2B — Primary)

| Revenue Stream | Buyer | What Christopher Provides | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaign setup fee | Research site or recruitment vendor | Set up approved outreach, distribution, and reporting | High |
| Monthly retainer | Research site, site network, or vendor | Ongoing audience access, campaign management, analytics | High |
| Fee per consented referral | Site, CRO, sponsor, or vendor | Prospect explicitly agrees to be contacted and is routed to official study team | High |
| Fee per completed prescreen | Site or vendor | Prospect completes approved prescreening process | Medium |
| Fee per screened/enrolled/randomized participant | Site, sponsor, CRO, or vendor | Outcome-based recruitment | Medium (post-contract) |
| White-label distribution partner | Recruitment agency | Access to opt-in niche audience | High after traction |
| Paid member tier | Individual consumers | Alerts, education, reminders, opportunity tracking | Low / supplemental |

**Market validation:** Antidote, SubjectWell/Reify, and Clariness operate around patient outreach, qualified referrals, prescreening, site support, and conversion. Their value is documented, consented, trackable referral quality — not just traffic.

## Compliant Automation Architecture

**Unsafe (original concept):**
```
Call → Voice memo → AI rewrite → Automatic Telegram post
```

**Safe (compliant version):**
```
Official recruitment call → Voice memo → AI transcription → Internal lead record
→ Request authorized materials → Written approval → Template-based draft
→ Human review → Publish
```

**What AI can safely automate:**
- Transcribe voice memo
- Extract fields (site name, condition, location, contact)
- Create internal CRM record
- Draft the approval-request email to the site

**What requires human action:**
- Requesting written authorization from the site/vendor
- Reviewing approved materials
- Clicking "publish" — every single time

## Safe "Publishability" Status System

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New call lead | Phone-derived, not for publication |
| Verification requested | Approval email sent to site/vendor |
| Approved asset received | Official flyer or copy in hand |
| Ready for review | Draft created, awaiting human review |
| Published | Live on Telegram |
| Paused | Temporarily suspended |
| Closed/expired | Recruitment complete or materials expired |

>

## Key Legal / Compliance Principles

- **Do NOT collect medical information** through Telegram, bots, Airtable, or unapproved forms. Use Telegram as a one-way alert channel only.
- **Compensation language:** Mention only when part of approved material. Never headline with payment. Do not promise or guarantee payment.
- **Referral fees:** Must be documented in a written services agreement. The study team retains all authority over screening, eligibility, consent, and enrollment.
- **Recording calls:** Check state recording-consent requirements before recording any CRO calls.
- **Get legal review before:** Signing B2B contracts, accepting outcome-based referral fees, collecting health data, performing study-specific prescreening, sending SMS at scale, or expanding across states.

---

## Long-Term Strategic Position

The defensible company is not a Telegram group. The long-term business becomes a **localized or niche participant recruitment infrastructure layer** with:
- A consented and segmented participant audience
- Verified research-site and sponsor relationships
- IRB-approved recruitment-material management
- Fast campaign distribution
- Secure routing to official study teams
- Source-to-referral analytics and performance reporting
- White-label services for established patient-recruitment vendors

**The key strategic shift:**
> Move from information arbitrage → authorized patient recruitment and referral infrastructure.
