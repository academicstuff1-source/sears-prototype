## 1. Operational Snapshot
Laura Booty is the founder and owner of LB Health Insurance Solutions. As an independent health insurance and Medicare broker in DFW, she represents multiple carriers, helping individuals, families, and small businesses navigate the complex health insurance landscape. 

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **The "Q4 Super Bowl" Dilemma:** Open enrollment for ACA (Nov 1) and Medicare (Oct 15) is weeks away. This is when independent brokers make 80% of their annual income. The friction is insane: phones ringing off the hook, chasing down drug lists, quoting across 5 different portals, and fielding frantic texts from clients asking "Does my plan still cover Dr. Smith?"
- **Friction Indicators:** When a broker is on a 45-minute enrollment call helping a senior citizen navigate Medicare Part D, they physically cannot answer the other three inbound calls coming in. If a new lead hits voicemail during open enrollment, they will just call the next broker on Google.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **[Missed OEP Leads]:** Missing a call during Open Enrollment Season is catastrophic to their bottom line, but they are too busy to answer every call.
2. **[Manual Document Chasing]:** Before a broker can run a quote, they have to collect the client's doctor list, prescription drug list, and a signed Scope of Appointment (SOA) form. Doing this manually via email or text is a massive bottleneck.
3. **[Repetitive FAQs]:** Field the same 10 questions every day ("What's my deductible?", "Is this covered?").
