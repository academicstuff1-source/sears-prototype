# 🎯 Account Intelligence: Clinic Concierge

**Target:** Lauren Payne & Shayla Dunlap (Co-Founders) | **Industry:** Mobile Wellness & Concierge Medicine | **Est. Size:** Boutique Co-Founders

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
Clinic Concierge was founded in 2021 by Lauren and Shayla. They operate a direct-pay (cash) concierge model in DFW, offering IV hydration, weight loss treatments, and sick visits. As co-founders of a boutique practice, they are likely wearing multiple hats—managing the clinical side while also handling the business, marketing, and scheduling.

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **The Direct-Pay Dynamic:** Because they don't take insurance, their entire business relies on high-touch patient experiences and rapid responses. 
- **Friction Indicators:** When two founders are managing a mobile/concierge practice, the administrative burden of patient intake, consent forms, and scheduling logistics often falls on their shoulders. If one is with a patient and the other is driving, a new prospect calling for a weight-loss consult might slip through the cracks.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **[The Intake Bottleneck]:** Concierge medicine requires heavy intake (medical histories, waivers, payment capture). Doing this manually via email or PDF before a mobile visit is incredibly tedious.
2. **[Speed to Lead]:** Similar to mobile IV, concierge sick visits are urgent. If a mother calls because her kid is sick and wants a home visit, a missed call means she's going to an urgent care clinic instead.
3. **[Founder Burnout]:** Managing the clinical care *and* the administrative follow-up usually leads to long nights doing paperwork.

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Lauren Payne or Shayla Dunlap** — Co-Founders

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (The Pre-Med/Concierge Hook):** "I'm a pre-med student building workflows for direct-pay clinics. I know you and Shayla are running a high-touch concierge model, which usually means the patient intake and scheduling falls entirely on your shoulders when you're already swamped with patients."
- **Angle B (Speed to Lead for Sick Visits):** "With mobile sick visits, speed is everything. I build systems that instantly text back missed calls and capture the patient's intake forms automatically, so you don't lose a booking to a local urgent care just because you couldn't answer the phone."
