# 🎯 Account Intelligence: First Texas Roofing

**Target:** Doug Maloney | **Industry:** Roofing (Residential/Commercial) | **Est. Size:** Owner-Operated

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
First Texas Roofing is an owner-operated roofing company based in Fort Worth, owned by Doug Maloney. The company prides itself on a "small company" culture. Doug is heavily and personally involved in almost every aspect of the business: providing quotes, managing job sites, and personally negotiating with insurance companies on behalf of clients. 

Because he is wearing all the hats (Sales, Operations, and Insurance Negotiator), his time is his most constrained resource.

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **Visible Tech Stack:** Unlikely to have a sophisticated CRM given the "small company" culture. He is using an `@att.net` email address for business, which strongly indicates a very manual, low-tech operational backend.
- **Friction Indicators:** When a major storm hits DFW, roofing is a race. If Doug is on a roof or on hold with an insurance adjuster, he cannot answer his phone. Every missed call during hail season is a lost $15,000+ roof replacement.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **[The Adjuster Bottleneck]:** Negotiating with insurance companies takes hours on the phone. While he is doing this, he cannot field new inbound quote requests.
2. **[Speed to Lead]:** In the roofing industry, the first person to show up usually gets the job. If he misses a call, that homeowner is calling the next roofer on Google.
3. **[Manual Follow-ups]:** Tracking which homeowners are waiting on insurance checks vs. which are ready to build requires airtight CRM discipline, which is hard to maintain from a pickup truck.

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Doug Maloney** — Owner (Primary target)

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (The Missed Call):** "Doug, since you're the one personally overseeing job sites and dealing with adjusters, what happens to a new lead that calls while you're on a roof?"
- **Angle B (Speed to Lead):** "During hail season, speed is everything. We set up systems that instantly text back missed calls and get their information so they don't call the next roofer on Google."
