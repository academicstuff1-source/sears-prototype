# 🎯 Account Intelligence: It's a Secret Med Spa

**Target URL:** secretmedspa.com | **Industry:** Med Spa | **Est. Size:** Multiple Locations (DFW, Houston, TX)

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
A rapidly growing medical spa with a modern, high-end brand aesthetic. They have scaled to multiple locations across major Texas metros (Dallas-Fort Worth and Houston). Their marketing is highly aggressive and visual, likely driving a huge volume of inbound DMs, texts, and web inquiries that must be triaged quickly before leads lose interest.

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **Visible Tech Stack:** Heavy Instagram/social media presence implies they use tools to manage social DMs (e.g., ManyChat, Sprout) alongside a core medical CRM (e.g., Aesthetic Record, PatientNow).
- **Friction Indicators:** Rapid multi-city expansion almost always outpaces operational infrastructure. With a heavy social media ad spend, their biggest bottleneck is likely "speed-to-lead" (qualifying and booking Instagram/TikTok leads before they bounce).

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **Social Media Lead Leakage:** When marketing generates 100 DMs over the weekend, front desk staff get overwhelmed on Monday morning. High risk of dropped leads.
2. **Standardized Experience Across Metros:** Ensuring the Houston clinic handles new patient onboarding exactly like the Dallas flagship. Manual QA on front desk performance is impossible at scale.

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Director of Marketing / Lead Generation** (Primary target - cares about lead conversion)
- **Chief Operating Officer / VP of Operations** (Secondary target - cares about scale)

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (Growth):** "Automating the triage and booking of inbound Instagram/social leads so your ad spend doesn't go to waste after hours."
- **Angle B (Efficiency):** "Centralizing patient communication so your front desk staff in DFW and Houston aren't manually texting back and forth for basic pricing questions."
