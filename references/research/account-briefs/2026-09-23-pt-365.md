# 🎯 Account Intelligence: Physical Therapy 365

**Target:** Jake Thomas, PT, DPT (Owner) | **Industry:** Mobile Physical Therapy | **Est. Size:** Solo Mobile Operator

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
Physical Therapy 365 is a DFW-based mobile outpatient PT practice owned by Jake Thomas. He provides care directly in patients' homes. 

This model is the ultimate bottleneck for an owner-operator. He is combining the two most friction-heavy workflows we've seen today:
1. **The Mobile Operator:** He is driving across DFW between appointments.
2. **The Clinical PT:** When he arrives, he is doing 45-60 minutes of uninterrupted, hands-on clinical care.

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **The "Impossible to Reach" Dilemma:** Because he is either driving or doing manual therapy, there is almost no time during the business day where he is sitting at a desk ready to answer a phone. 
- **Friction Indicators:** The website explicitly states "Phone/Text: 945-209-1854," which means his primary intake channel is his cell phone. If a patient calls to book an evaluation and he is stretching a patient's hamstring, that call goes to VM.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **[The Windshield Time Tax]:** When he gets back to his car after a session, he has to spend 15 minutes returning voicemails and texting patients intake forms before he can drive to the next house.
2. **[Missed Cash Revenue]:** Just like the IV guys, a mobile PT session is a premium service. A missed call is lost revenue.
3. **[Manual Intake on the Road]:** Trying to email PDFs or link to a patient portal while sitting in a hot car in a driveway in Farmers Branch.

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Jake Thomas** — Owner / DPT (Primary target)

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (The Windshield Tax):** "Jake, as a pre-med student, I know that doing mobile PT means you're either driving or doing manual therapy, which means you have zero time to answer your phone. I build systems that text back your missed calls while you're in a session."
