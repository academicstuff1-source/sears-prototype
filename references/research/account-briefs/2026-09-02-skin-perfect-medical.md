## 1. Operational Snapshot
A major player in the Southern California medical aesthetics market. With at least 6 locations ranging from Los Angeles to Orange County and the Inland Empire, they operate at a scale resembling a corporate franchise. They offer weight loss, laser, injectables, and wellness services. 

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **Visible Tech Stack:** At this scale, they likely use enterprise-grade medical spa software (Zenoti, Nextech) and a centralized call center or heavily loaded local reception teams.
- **Friction Indicators:** Managing staff turnover at the front desk across 6 locations in California is incredibly expensive. Training new receptionists on 50+ treatments and their pre-requisites creates constant friction.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **Call Volume & Triaging:** Patients calling to ask if a specific laser treatment is right for them. Receptionists are not medical providers and often struggle to answer clinical FAQs, leading to unnecessary consultations or frustrated callers.
2. **After-Hours Booking:** In SoCal's competitive market, if a patient can't get an immediate answer at 8 PM on a Tuesday, they will book with a competitor.

- **Regional Operations Director** (Primary target)
- **Practice Administrator** (Secondary target)
