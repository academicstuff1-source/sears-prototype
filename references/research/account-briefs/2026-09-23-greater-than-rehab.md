# 🎯 Account Intelligence: Greater Than Performance & Rehab

**Target:** Scott Pollock (Founder / Owner) | **Industry:** Cash-Based Physical Therapy | **Est. Size:** Solo/Boutique Clinic

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
Greater Than Performance & Rehab is a Dallas-based clinic founded by Scott Pollock. It operates on a "cash-based" or performance model. This means they do not rely on insurance reimbursements. They are highly reliant on high-quality patient experiences, word-of-mouth, and converting inbound leads into paying cash clients.

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **The "Hands-On" Dilemma:** In cash-based PT, the therapist spends a full 45-60 minutes hands-on with a single patient (unlike insurance clinics that juggle 3 patients at once). This means Scott is physically unable to answer his phone or check his email for an entire hour at a time.
- **Friction Indicators:** Because it's cash-pay, every lead is high-value. If a prospect calls while Scott is with a patient, and they hit a generic voicemail, they are highly likely to call the next clinic on Google. Furthermore, sending intake forms to cash-pay patients requires airtight follow-up to ensure they fill them out before arriving.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **[Missed Calls = Lost Cash Revenue]:** If a patient is willing to pay out-of-pocket for PT, they want help *now*. Missing their call means losing a $150-$250 session and a potential $1,500+ plan of care.
2. **[Manual Intake Workflows]:** Getting patients to fill out comprehensive health histories via PDF or basic web forms is frustrating, and therapists hate chasing patients for paperwork.
3. **[Patient Reactivation]:** Cash-pay patients tend to "drop off" when they feel 80% better. Following up with them manually to get them back in the door is a chore most solo PTs neglect.

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Scott Pollock** — Founder / DPT (Primary target)

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (The Missed Call in Session):** "Scott, as a pre-med guy, I know that in cash-based PT, you are spending a full hour hands-on with a patient. What happens to a new patient lead who calls to book while you're in the middle of a dry needling session?"
- **Angle B (Reactivation Automation):** "Cash-pay patients are notorious for dropping off after 4 sessions when they feel 'good enough.' I build systems that automatically check in with them 3 weeks later and get them to re-book, so you don't have to manually text them."
