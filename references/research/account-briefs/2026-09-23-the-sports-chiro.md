# 🎯 Account Intelligence: The Sports Chiro

**Target:** Dr. Chris Ward (Owner/Founder) | **Industry:** Cash-Based Chiropractic / Sports Rehab | **Est. Size:** Solo Operator

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
The Sports Chiro is a private, cash-based clinic serving the Plano/Frisco area, owned and operated by Dr. Chris Ward. He explicitly advertises his model as "cash-based by design with one-on-one sessions." 

Because he is cash-based, he is not doing 5-minute insurance adjustments where he rushes patients in and out. He is likely doing 30-to-60-minute manual therapy and rehab sessions.

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **The "Hands-On" Dilemma:** Similar to cash PTs, a cash-based sports chiropractor is constantly doing manual adjustments, soft tissue work, or guiding rehab exercises. He cannot answer his phone during these long sessions.
- **Friction Indicators:** He has to convince people to pay out-of-pocket instead of using their insurance. This requires a high level of customer service and instant responsiveness. A prospect who hits his voicemail will likely just call an in-network chiropractor down the street.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **[Missed Calls = Lost Cash Revenue]:** If an athlete hurts their back squatting and calls him, they want to book immediately. Missing that call means losing a high-value cash patient.
2. **[Manual Intake Workflows]:** Getting cash-pay patients to sign waivers and fill out their sports injury history before they arrive is tedious to track manually.
3. **[Reactivation]:** Athletes drop off when their pain goes away. Following up to get them in for "maintenance" sessions is usually done manually via text, or ignored completely.

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Dr. Chris Ward** — Owner / Chiropractor (Primary target)

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (The Missed Call):** "Dr. Ward, as a pre-med student, I know that in a cash-based sports practice, you're doing long, one-on-one sessions. What happens to a new lead who calls to book while you're in the middle of a 45-minute rehab session?"
