# 🎯 Account Intelligence: Concierge Physical Therapists

**Target URL:** conciergephysicaltherapists.com | **Industry:** Mobile/In-Home PT | **Est. Size:** DFW Region

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
A mobile, in-home physical therapy service operating across Dallas and Fort Worth. Eliminates the physical clinic entirely. 

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **Visible Tech Stack:** Heavy reliance on mobile EMR, routing/scheduling software, and digital communication tools.
- **Friction Indicators:** Mobile therapists have zero front-desk support in the field. Every administrative task they do cuts into their driving/treatment time.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **[Therapist Admin Burden]:** Therapists having to manually text patients ETAs, send intake forms from their cars, or upload notes.
2. **[Routing/Scheduling Inefficiency]:** Handling last-minute changes manually across a widespread metroplex like DFW.

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Operations Director / Owner**

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (Efficiency/Retention):** "Automating appointment reminders, ETAs, and digital intake so field therapists never have to do admin work from their cars."
