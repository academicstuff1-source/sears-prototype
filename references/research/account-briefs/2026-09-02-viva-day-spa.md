# 🎯 Account Intelligence: Viva Day Spa + Med Spa

**Target URL:** vivadayspa.com | **Industry:** Med Spa & Day Spa | **Est. Size:** 3+ Locations (Austin, Dallas, Round Rock)

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
A highly-rated, multi-location hybrid day spa and med spa based in Texas. They offer traditional spa services alongside medical aesthetic treatments (Botox, fillers, laser). Their dual model means they handle a massive volume of distinct appointment types—ranging from a 60-minute massage to clinical injectables—each requiring different intake forms, pre-care instructions, and practitioner credentials.

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **Visible Tech Stack:** Likely using a complex booking system like Zenoti, Mindbody, or Boulevard that attempts to handle both standard spa and medical scheduling.
- **Friction Indicators:** Managing distinct intake workflows (medical vs. non-medical) across multiple locations is notoriously difficult. If a client books online, they often have to complete redundant paperwork upon arrival. 

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **Intake & Paperwork Fragmentation:** Coordinating medical history forms versus standard spa waivers. If front-desk staff have to manually reconcile forms before the appointment, this is a massive time sink.
2. **Cross-Selling & Follow-Up Automation:** Med spa services have high recurring value. Are they effectively tracking when a Botox client is due for a refill and automating that outreach, or is it manual?

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Operations Director / Regional Manager** (Primary target)
- **Founders / Owners** (Secondary target)

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (Efficiency):** "Streamlining medical vs. day spa intake workflows so your front desk isn't chasing down clipboards across your Austin and Dallas locations."
- **Angle B (Growth/Retention):** "Automating post-care follow-ups and 90-day re-booking reminders for high-margin injectable clients."
