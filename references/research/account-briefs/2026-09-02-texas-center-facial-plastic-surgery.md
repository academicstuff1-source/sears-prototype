# 🎯 Account Intelligence: Texas Center for Facial Plastic Surgery (Med Spa)

**Target URL:** uth.edu (or affiliated private clinic URL) | **Industry:** Plastic Surgery & Med Spa | **Est. Size:** Large Surgical/Aesthetic Hybrid (Houston)

---

## 1. Operational Snapshot
A high-end, medically rigorous practice combining facial plastic surgery with non-surgical med spa treatments (Botox, fillers, lasers). These hybrid practices have the highest patient lifetime value in the industry, as med spa patients eventually become surgical patients, and surgical patients require med spa maintenance.

## 2. Tech & Friction Signals
- **Visible Tech Stack:** Likely using highly secure, HIPAA-compliant EHRs like Nextech or Symplast, which are incredibly powerful but notoriously clunky for marketing and patient follow-up.
- **Friction Indicators:** High-end surgical practices often treat their med spa wing as a secondary priority. Front desk staff are focused on high-ticket surgical patients, leaving $500 Botox leads waiting for a callback.

## 3. High-Probability Pain Points
1. **The Surgical / Med Spa Divide:** Front desk staff prioritizing a $20k surgical consult over a $500 lip filler inquiry, causing massive leakage in the med spa pipeline.
2. **Post-Op / Post-Treatment Follow-up:** Doctors want 24-hour and 7-day check-ins for quality of care, but staff forget to make the calls.

## 4. Key Decision Makers
- **Practice Administrator / Managing Director** (Primary target)
- **Lead Plastic Surgeon / Owner** (Secondary target - cares about patient experience)

## 5. Recommended Strategic Angles
- **Angle A (Growth):** "Automating the triage of med spa leads so your front desk can focus entirely on white-glove service for high-ticket surgical patients."
- **Angle B (Efficiency):** "Implementing automated 24-hour and 7-day post-treatment SMS check-ins that flag adverse reactions and escalate to clinical staff only when necessary."
