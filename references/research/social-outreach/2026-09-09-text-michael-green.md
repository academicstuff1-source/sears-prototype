## The Strategy (Give First, Ask Later)
When a busy contact ghosts a meeting request, the best play is to offer a "free sample" of the value asynchronously. We use the **Permission to Send** framework: you don't send the video outright, you ask permission to send it. A "yes" is a micro-commitment that re-opens the conversation.
