# Prospecting Research SOP

**Purpose:** Provide a fast, repeatable workflow for qualifying target accounts, gathering contact data using free-tier tools, and preparing for customized outreach in under 30 minutes.

**Prerequisites:**
- Free-tier Prospeo account (manual UI search)
- Free-tier Clay account (manual UI search)
- HubSpot CRM Free

---

## Stage 0: The Tiering Gate (30 seconds)
Before spending time researching a company, apply the tier filter. If they do not pass, discard and move on.

1. **Tier 1 (Warm Community):** Pre-existing connection (church, Fortrea, direct referral). **PROCEED.**
2. **Tier 2 (Owner-Operated SMB):** Boutique services, trades, local pros. Owner answers the phone. **PROCEED.**
3. **Tier 3 (Healthcare Clinic / Practitioner):** High gatekeeper friction. **STOP.** Do not approach cold. Only proceed if you have a Tier 1 warm introduction.
4. **Tier 4 (Corporate / Enterprise):** High IT lockout. **STOP.** Discard.

---

## Stage 1: Basic Qualification (5 minutes)
Do a quick Google search and scan their website. Look for 3 out of 4 "Yes" answers:

- [ ] Is the owner or key decision-maker identifiable by name on the "About" page or LinkedIn?
- [ ] Is the business actively operating? (Recent Google reviews, functioning website)
- [ ] Is the business small enough that the owner is likely involved in operations? (Under ~25 employees)
- [ ] Are there visible pain signals? (E.g., No online booking, bad reviews complaining about slow communication, actively hiring for front-desk/admin roles).

*If yes to 3+, proceed to Stage 2.*

---

## Stage 2: Prospeo Manual Lookup (5–10 minutes)
1. Log into your free Prospeo account.
2. Search for the target company URL or the specific owner's name + company.
3. Extract the verified email address and/or direct phone number.
4. **Red Flag Check:** If you can only find a general "info@" email or the main office phone line, note this as a high gatekeeper risk.

---

## Stage 3: Clay Enrichment (Optional, 5 minutes)
*Note: Do NOT use paid credits.*
1. Log into Clay (free tier).
2. Input the company domain or owner's LinkedIn URL.
3. Surface any free contextual signals: recent company news, hiring activity, or LinkedIn posts by the owner.
4. If the free tier yields nothing useful, skip and move on.

---

## Stage 4: Pain Hypothesis Generation (5 minutes)
Based on what you found, construct a "Why Change? Why Now?" hypothesis.
- **Why Change?** What is the cost of their current manual process? (e.g., lost leads, staff burnout).
- **Why Now?** Did they recently expand? Are they hiring?
- **Strategic Angle:** Pick one:
  - *Growth:* "How to handle expansion without doubling admin headcount."
  - *Efficiency:* "Plugging holes in patient/client intake so you spend less time on admin."

---

## Stage 5: Build the Account Brief (5 minutes)
1. Open the [prospect-research-brief-template.md](../examples/prospect-research-brief-template.md).
2. Fill in the extracted data, tech signals, and your pain hypothesis.
3. Save the completed file in `references/research/account-briefs/` using the format `YYYY-MM-DD-[company-slug].md`.

---

## Stage 6: HubSpot CRM Logging (5 minutes)
1. Open HubSpot Free.
2. Create a new Contact record for the decision-maker.
3. Attach or paste the Account Brief into the contact's Notes.
4. Set Pipeline Stage to: `New Prospect`.
5. Log the planned outreach method (Warm text, Cold call, LinkedIn message, Email).
6. **Critical:** Set a specific Follow-Up Task with a date (e.g., "Execute cold call on Thursday 10 AM").

---
*Workflow Complete. The target is now staged and ready for outreach execution.*
