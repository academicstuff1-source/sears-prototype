## Ideal Prospect Profile & Targeting Strategy (V1)
*Based on early traction data (Sept 22, 2026), cold outreach to healthcare practitioners yields high gatekeeper friction and low receptivity. Outreach is now tiered:*

1. **Tier 1 (Highest Priority):** Warm community ties (church network, former Fortrea colleagues, referrals). Pre-existing social trust bypasses friction.
2. **Tier 2:** Owner-operated small businesses (boutique services, local professional services, local trades). The owner answers the phone and owns the problem.
3. **Tier 3 (Avoid Cold):** Healthcare practitioner offices (med spas, dentists, clinics). Do not approach cold. Only approach if there is a warm Tier 1 introduction.
4. **Tier 4 (Avoid):** Corporate/Enterprise. Too much IT lockout and gatekeeping for current stage.

## Success Criteria
