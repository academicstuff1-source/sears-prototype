# 1-Page Offer Summary (Draft v1)

> **Goal:** Keep this simple enough to explain in one sentence, and specific enough that a prospect understands exactly what they are buying.

## 1. The Core Elevator Pitch
"I build the automated 'glue' that connects your social media ads directly to your booking system, so your front desk never drops another weekend lead."

## 2. Customer Type
- **Primary Target:** Multi-location aesthetic clinics and Med Spas (3+ locations).
- **Secondary Indicator:** High monthly spend on social media ads (Instagram/TikTok) but relying on front-desk staff to manually answer DMs and texts.

## 3. The Problem
Heavy, closed-loop EHRs (like Zenoti or Mindbody) are great for checking in physical patients, but they are terrible at top-of-funnel marketing. When a clinic runs ads, the inbound DMs and texts overwhelm the front desk. Leads sit unanswered for 4-8 hours. The marketing budget is wasted because the "Speed-to-Lead" is broken.

## 4. The Outcome (What they get)
- **Zero Lead Leakage:** Every inbound social inquiry gets an immediate, AI-assisted triage response (24/7).
- **Staff Freedom:** Front-desk receptionists stop acting as data-entry clerks and focus 100% on the patients standing in the lobby.
- **Maximized ROI:** Higher conversion rate on existing ad spend.

## 5. Scope & Delivery (The "Product")
**Phase 1: The "Speed-to-Lead" Audit (Free or Low-Cost Foot-in-the-Door)**
- 15-minute screen share or lobby visit.
- Output: A visual map showing exactly where their leads are falling through the cracks between Instagram and their EHR.

**Phase 2: The Core Implementation (The Paid Engagement)**
- Set up an automated webhook/API connection (using Make/Zapier/custom scripts).
- Capture inbound leads (social/web) and automatically push them into their CRM/EHR as qualified targets.
- Set up an automated SMS follow-up sequence for unresponsive leads.
- Delivery Time: 7 to 10 days.

## 6. Pricing Hypothesis
- **Audit/Discovery:** Free (Used as the lead-magnet/meeting closer).
- **Core Implementation (Setup Fee):** $1,500 - $2,500 one-time build fee.
- **Maintenance/Retainer (Optional, Later):** $250/mo for ongoing prompt adjustments, Zapier/Make tier costs, and troubleshooting.

*Note: This is a working hypothesis. We will adjust the scope and pricing based on the next 4 discovery conversations.*
