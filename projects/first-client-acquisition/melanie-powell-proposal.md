## What I'm Proposing

### Component 1 — Post-Discovery Follow-Up Sequence

When a prospect leaves a discovery call without committing, a structured sequence runs automatically — no action required from you.

| Timing | Action | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Personalized value-add email | Reinforce the fit, address common hesitations, provide a useful resource |
| Day 3 | Soft SMS check-in | Short, warm, low-pressure nudge ("Still thinking things over?") |
| Day 7 | Objection-handling email | Address the most common reasons prospects delay (comparing doulas, cost, timing) |
| Ongoing | Lead continues to be nurtured | System keeps the relationship warm without you having to remember |
| When ready | Hot lead alert to you | You get a notification when a prospect re-engages — that's your cue to step back in |

**The result:** Ghosted leads get a second chance. You only re-enter the conversation when someone is warm again — not to chase.

---

### Component 2 — Lead Magnet Funnel Tightening

Your existing 3-email welcome sequence is a solid foundation. I'd review and extend it to make sure:

- The emails flow logically from awareness → interest → booking intent
- There's a clear, low-friction call to action in each email
- The handoff from the last welcome email to a discovery call booking is smooth and automatic
- No leads fall through the gap between "opened an email" and "booked a call"

---

## What This Looks Like in Practice

From your side, the workflow becomes:

1. Lead fills out your form → welcome sequence runs automatically ✓  
2. Lead books a discovery call → you show up and do what you do best ✓  
3. Lead says "I need to think about it" → follow-up sequence runs automatically ✓  
4. Lead re-engages → you get a notification and pick up the conversation ✓  

You stay focused on care. The system handles the in-between.
